# Owner constraints — Soumaya Environment Production

Binding on every stage. The mission brief (owner, 2026-10-31) plus the
owner's own words in chat outrank any document in the repo.

## Eternity (owner, 2026-10-31, verbatim): "Keep eternity his GOB —
## his world around him can look different but keep his GOB"

- **Eternity himself does NOT change.** His figure is his GOB — his
  established god-form: male, bare-skinned, all white, no face, no
  hair, no body detail, thin golden ring behind his head, cosmic
  scale by default. Never redesign, re-clothe, re-face, feminize,
  or re-interpret him; no religious symbols; he is not a figure from
  a real-world religion.
- **The world AROUND him may be reinterpreted** — the white void,
  distant colored shell, neon veils, liquid-dye clouds, the floating
  speech tablet, visitor ships, orbit and scale relationships may
  be rebuilt to production quality — as long as he remains the
  dominant presence, the colors stay distant, and no floor, temple,
  throne room, or ordinary architecture is introduced.

## Standing mission restrictions (from the brief, §11)

- No lore changes, no invented story facts, no removed locations,
  no changed world identities, no renamed anchors.
- No commits, pushes, merges, deploys. No overwriting production
  GLBs. No Three.js / app code changes. Blender production +
  preview artifacts only; integration is a separate authorized
  stage.
- No assets with unclear licensing; provenance recorded for all.
- Mysteries stay mysteries: nothing beyond the village doorway,
  nothing explaining the seventh hand, no reveal of what the First
  Morning was defended from, the road's end never appears, chalk is
  strokes never letters.
