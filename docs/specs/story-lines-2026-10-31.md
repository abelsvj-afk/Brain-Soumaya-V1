# The Lines of keeping — the Book's lineage view (2026-10-31)

Draft 7 §4 names seven **Lines** (teacher → student → heir). The Book gets a quiet second view of them. Pure presentation of what is already found; nothing stored, nothing new to earn.

- A **person lights** when the brain has found ANY entry that is theirs (`StoryLine.people[].entries`). Until then they are only a shape — their name is not shown (no spoilers by list).
- A Line's **thread lights** when at least two of its people are lit. That is the only "progress" cue and it never shows a count, ratio, "complete" or "missing".
- Lines with no lit person are shown as a single unnamed shape. A Line never says how many people it has.
- Tests pin: every entry id is real; no digits/banned words; every Line has ≥ 2 people (so a thread can exist); ids unique.
- UI: components/BookLines.tsx under the Roads list; list semantics, state in words ("a thread joins them"), shape + text, never colour alone; reduced-motion has nothing to calm (no motion).
