# Seam S-VIII — The Folded Letter (spec; the last seam, built under the owner's standing "keep building everything that's not built", 2026-10-31)

Draft 7 §6: *silence:* the one sentence · *region:* an "ending" region the player reaches only by choice · *cast:* the Folded One · *adds:* the sentence's **reader** (never its words) · **must never say whether it is kind.** Draft 3 #14: the Folded One wrote a letter to her and never sent it; the fold holds the permission to keep without opening; if ever opened it reads one sentence, never shown. Draft 4: the Folded One's memory is an empty room with a letter on the floor, still folded. Draft 7: the Archivist, if you do not open the fold, thanks you for the thing she needed most.

## Shape
- A new flight world `the-folded-letter`: a small bare room open to the night. One letter folded in thirds in the middle of the floor; an empty cushion in a pool of lamplight facing it, the size of whoever comes (the *reader*, shown only as a place); a lamp left lit and topped by no one seen; a chair drawn back from a table holding a capped pen and no paper; a long shelf of kept letters, none opened, each with one thumb's press; a window onto more stars than the room has walls; a door ajar one hand's breadth, never shut; a seat by the door worn by long sitting. No figure; no text, and no readable mark on any paper.
- **Reached only by choice:** the door opens only for a brain holding the Unopened Fold (`relic.unopened-fold`), is on the board only then, and nothing leads or invites there (its blurb says "There is no reason to go. You may go, and you may leave when you like."). Owner exempt; warp by chart distance (chart 600,760, the furthest point; worst leg 19); drift and home free. **The choice is real and the letter is never shown** (see §Choice below; this replaces the first version, which offered no way to open it — audit gap G2).
- 10 Book entries, 8 place passages, 4 Item Reactions, one road ("A Thing Kept Without Opening", the Archivist). The Archivist gives a disc of smooth seal-wax after five meetings and thanks you; the Archivist, Ysmerel, Calend and Tamsin roam into it.
- Art: `docs/lore/art/PROMPT_SHEET_10_THE_FOLDED_LETTER.md` for Muse.

## Rules it keeps
Never shows or paraphrases the sentence; never says whether it is kind; never rewards, scores or counts opening or not opening (the server keeps only *which*, privately, never any words); nobody is shown; the door is always ajar and the room "will think no less of you"; reduced motion holds the lamp still; the gate must not strand.

## Choice (added after the playability audit, gap G2)
In the room, the letter's "Look closely" card offers **Keep it folded** or **Open it**. *Open it* asks once more ("It will be read once, by you, and cannot be unread. The room will not say what it says.") with **Not yet** beside it; opening is final. *Keep it folded* is not final — you may open it later, or never. Either way the card never shows the sentence and says plainly that the room will not say what it said **or whether it was kind**; a voice from the seat by the door answers (kept: "Thank you. That was the thing I needed most." / opened: nothing is asked, a kettle is put on). Server: only which (`kept`/`opened`) is stored, privately, in `story_roads` under the reserved id `choice.fold-letter`, and only while the brain is in the room. Nothing is counted, scored, rewarded or unlocked by either choice.
Gate: the Unopened Fold, **or** the open alcove together with Vesh's paper boat (the Fold's relic is not yet discoverable in the app — audit gap G1).
