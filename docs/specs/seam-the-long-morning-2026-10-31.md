## Shape (same machinery as the other seams)
- A new flight world `the-long-morning`: warm light with no horizon — floor, fog and sky are one colour, so the ground simply ends in light (the host skips the shared starfield for it, as for the garden and Eternity). A whole ring of tall pale leaves ruled with fine lines of light and no gap anywhere; one blank leaf the size of a table at the middle; a flake of the first light on a plain stand; a basin of still light; loose groups of tall faceless columns of soft light that lean a little toward a still visitor; a home of four posts with a lamp trimmed for company; a groove across the ground with no beginning; a curve of light that stops. No figure, no text.
