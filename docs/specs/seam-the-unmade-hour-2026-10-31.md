# Seam S-I — The Unmade Hour (spec; built under the owner's standing "keep building everything that's not built", 2026-10-31)

Draft 7 §6: *silence:* "the hour made twice" (the Sixth Hour) · *region:* the first Sixth Hour, before the second · *cast:* the first Mercy; the made who weren't kept · *adds:* what unmaking felt like from inside · **must never say why the hour was made twice.**

## Shape (same machinery as the Ward, the Road and the Village)
- A new flight world `the-unmade-hour`: a wide pale floor that keeps no marks of visitors. Shallow hollows come to a ring from every side and stop; at the ring's centre one place is worn smooth by standing, with a small unlit lamp; seven stone hands, palms up, six holding a small light and the seventh the same hollow with nothing in it; a frame the size of a mirror with no glass; a low wall cut with finger-wide strokes in groups, the last group open; a step across the floor exactly one hand thick; a held note of faint light. No figure is ever shown; no text.
- Gate = a door in the story: opens for a brain that has found the Lullaby's Hollow (`sight.lullaby-hollow`, the soft gap along Vesh's hull). Owner exempt; warp by chart distance (chart 110,420; worst leg 19); drift and home free.
- 10 Book entries, 8 place passages, 4 Item Reactions, one road ("What the Floor Keeps", Ysmerel). The Archivist gives a leaf "set down, not burned" after five meetings; the Archivist and Ysmerel roam into it.
- Art: `docs/lore/art/PROMPT_SHEET_6_THE_UNMADE_HOUR.md` for Muse.

## Rules it keeps
Never says why the hour was made twice; never shows or names the made who were not kept (only the shape of where they were); the seventh hand is not explained; no digits shown; the step is "one hand thick" (the Deep Past's missing stratum echo); reduced motion holds the held note still; the gate must not strand.
