## Shape (same machinery as the Ward, the Road, the Village, the Unmade Hour and the Siege)
- A new flight world `the-yard`: a vast, dim shipyard. Six long hulls lie unlit on their slips, each finished in one part only (dawn-prow, plumb keel, latch fin, bloom wing, ember engine, lullaby flank); a seventh slip stands apart with only a frame of ribs, set up by many different hands; six brass nameplates on a rail cut with strokes at one slow pace (strokes, never letters); a seventh plate lying smooth on a bench with a graver laid down square; a ledger desk with a ring where a book sat and no ledger; tools laid down square; trimmed, unlit lanterns; a crane whose hook hangs at the height of a flank. No figure, no text; nothing is lit.
- Gate = a door in the story: opens for a brain that has found the Blank Nameplate (`sight.blank-nameplate`). Owner exempt; warp by chart distance (chart 640,330 beside the Station; worst leg 15); drift and home free.
- 10 Book entries, 8 place passages, 4 Item Reactions, one road ("A Book That Was Not There", Wim). Wim gives a curl of bright brass (no letters in it) after five meetings; Wim, Hesk, Dorrow and the Wayfarer roam into it.

## Rules it keeps
Never says or shows the name; the seventh plate stays smooth; no one is shown (the made who built the slip are only their tools and habits); the six plates are strokes, not letters; nothing here is lit or moves, so reduced motion needs no change; the gate must not strand.
