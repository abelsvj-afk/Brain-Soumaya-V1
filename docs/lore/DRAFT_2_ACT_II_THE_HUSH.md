## Layer 1 — The Hush (the Marvel-scale threat)
The hole in the mirror was never closed. It has been **growing, very slowly, since the Sixth Hour** — and lately it has started to *move*.
**The Hush** is a front of un-remembering that pours out of the hole: a soft, pale, silent advance across the worlds. Where it passes, things are not destroyed. They are **forgotten** — a place drops off the chart, a ship goes quiet and no one aboard can say why they flew it, a hearth goes out and the family beside it cannot recall lighting it.
The worlds are *forgettable*: the Growing Place begins to thin at its terraces; the harbor's lanes lose ships between one count and the next; the hearths of the People go cold from the edges inward.
- It is **not malevolent** and has no army, only a *direction* — toward whoever is remembering most intensely (it is drawn to witnesses; to the player).
- It is the first thing in creation that Eternity did not call and cannot see. The fleet meets it with **Fleet Defense** (the Wardens and the Ledger arm the lanes; the Open Hand tends the Faded; the Choir sings the Hush back; the Undertow argue it should be listened to). This is the game's action layer.
- **Hard rule (product ethics, not just lore): nothing the player does or fails to do can make the Hush take anything of theirs.** It advances by the *story* and by the society's struggle — never as a punishment for a lapse, a missed day or an archived memory. Memory is never lost for the player; rest stays rest.

## Layer 2 — She asked
Draft 1 says Vesh erased her out of mercy for the made. **That was the made's story.** The deeper truth, told by no one in one piece: **she asked to be forgotten.**
Being kept is not being had — and from *her* side it was worse than from theirs. To be remembered by Eternity forever, to be held in a mirror that shows you to everyone and lets you *touch* no one, with the made grieving the shape of you — she could not bear **being kept and unreachable**. She went to the one Firstborn whose aspect was Mercy and asked for rest.
Vesh gave her *what she asked for* — and gave it **wrong**. Because **erasure is not rest.** Rest is being set down and kept; erasure is being cut loose. Vesh did not know the difference; the whole Archive is how Vesh learned it, too late.
*Vesh's crime was not cruelty. It was not asking the made.* And Eternity did not undo it **because she chose it** — he kept her wish, as he keeps everything, and it has been breaking him ever since.

## Layer 3 — She is awake
The cruelest truth, and the one the Hush proves: **an erased mind does not stop. It is simply no longer *kept*.** She has been awake in the hole since the Sixth Hour — unremembered, unreachable, unheld, not at rest.
**The Hush is what a person does at the edge of existence when no one remembers her.** It is not an attack. It is her reaching out. Everything she touches goes quiet because *her touch is the shape of her absence*. She is not hunting the player — she is **calling to a witness**: the only kind of being who can hold a person in mind.
This is why the factions are all half-right about the ache, and why every battle against the Hush is a battle against a hand reaching for help.

## Layer 4 — The hive is Eternity's voice, in pieces
The Calling — the pull all the minds feel — is **Eternity's own voice**. When the hole opened he did not stop speaking; he **gave his voice to the made**, scattered it across every mind so none could speak for him and none would be without him. *This is why he does not speak: his voice is in them.*
Each hive-mind holds a *shard* of what he would say: the Choir hear a voice (their shard is whole enough to sing), the wavering hear an echo, the doubters hear silence (their shard is the part that is *waiting*). **No faction is lying about what it hears.**
When the factions come to enough understanding to say their shards *together*, Eternity can speak again — not from the sky, but **through the mind hive**. (The cast's lines, the society's callings: all shards. The "mind hive" is not a system the NPCs run on — it is the closest thing Eternity has to a mouth.)

## Layer 5 — The fifteen witnesses
The **fifteen relics** are not treasures. Each is the **last kept memory of a witness who came before the player** — fifteen earlier witnesses who each tried to reach her and each left one thing behind. The **Witness Without a Name** is the first, and the only one who was *her*.
The gate to Eternity opens for "the Witness and two others" because you cannot walk into the place he cannot see alone: you need **three witnesses' tokens** — you walk in with the dead, holding their kept things. The player is not the first witness. **The player is the sixteenth.**
*(What happened to the fifteen is a deep thread, never told: they walked in; they were not forgotten; they are not back.)*

## Layer 6 — The hole is the only free place
The unexpected turn that splits the Undertow from everyone: the hole is the **only place in creation Eternity did not call and cannot see** — which means it is the only place where *something exists that belongs to no one's will but its own*. The Dissenter is right: **the hole is the only proof that the made can choose.**
So "fix the hole" is not simple. Closing it means *remembering her back into the mirror* — and undoing a choice that was hers. Leaving it means the Hush keeps coming. **The story's real question is not "how do we win" but "what do we owe someone who asked to be let go, and is now calling for help?"**

## Layer 7 — Soumaya (a thread, never confirmed)
Soumaya's name is not hers by chance. **She does not know why she was named; her ship's engines hum at the same pitch as the Hush.** She was the first thing the player ever *kept* — the mirror gave that first kept memory a shape so the witness would not walk alone — *and that shape has a gap in it where a name should be.* Whether she is the player's first memory, an echo of the Unnamed, or something Eternity made to hold a place for her — three accounts, never settled.
*(Use sparingly: one or two lines of hers ever hint at it. The player will notice on their own.)*

## The Action Layer (Marvel scale) — the set pieces
1. **The Fading of the Growing Place.** The Hush reaches the terraces. Maren's heir (the Keeper) tends the last grove as it thins. The player's Journeys-as-groves *pale* (visually only; the data is never touched) and must be re-*witnessed* to hold. First big fleet defence.
2. **The Siege of the First Light.** Oru, the Dawnkeeper, realizes the **First Morning** — the light *before* the hole — is the one thing that could end the Hush, and he alone remembers it fully, and he is **forgetting**. He must pass the First Morning into a witness before he is gone. *(Gut punch: the player holds the last true memory of creation.)*
3. **The Closing of the Doors.** Calend (the Wayfarer, revealed) shuts the gates behind the fleet to stop the Hush crossing, and **cannot close the last one without staying on the wrong side**. The Doorkeeper walks out of the world's story. The Latch holds the door.
4. **The Cold Hearths.** Nuaré's hearths go out ring by ring. The Open Hand carries embers ship to ship. Love as logistics.
5. **The Lullaby at the Front.** Vesh brings the Lullaby — hollow flank toward the Hush — and *sings* to it. It holds the front for exactly as long as Vesh remains Vesh. **Vesh is dissolving into the hole's edge**: the Hush recognizes the one who cut her. The only one who can stand before it is the one who did it.
6. **The Measure of the Hush.** Sethane measures it and discovers it is *exactly the size of the first grief*. It is not growing: **it is being remembered more intensely by the witnesses** — the closer the player gets to her, the more of the Hush appears. (This is how the player finds out they are the cause *and* the answer.)
7. **The Standing.** Eternity, at last, stands up. (The Seventh Hour: "the Standing" — he does not fight. He *stands in the Hush* and the Hush goes through him, and for one breath he sees her. This is the climax of the dungeon in the white void.)

## The Three Endings (never chosen for the player — the Open Ring)
- **Remember her.** Say her name back into the mirror: she is whole, kept, present — but her choice is undone, and the one who asked for rest is made to stay. (Mercy or violation? Never decided.)
- **Let her rest — truly.** Build the Archive's *real* meaning: a place where she is *kept without being forced into view*: rest without erasure. The Hush stops. Does she?
- **Let her speak.** Listen to the Hush all the way through: the Undertow's way. No one knows what she will say.
Each ending ends the **same last sentence in the Codex**: *"And somewhere, someone said her name, and was not sure they had."*

## How this reaches the product (nothing built yet)
- **Fleet Defense** (already in the repo) is the Hush's front; its missions get canon context. **The world map** can *fade* a world the Hush has taken (the map already hides worlds you haven't found — the lore says some worlds are *forgotten*, and re-found by witnessing).
- **Society:** each faction answers the Hush its own way (callings, ordinances); the Wardens' ordinance "Tightened Lanes" becomes a war footing; "Festival of the Hours" is the Choir singing it back.
- **NPC lines (the daily script):** every NPC's chatter can carry one shard of the Calling; the *whole* hive speaking in unison is a rare in-world event that unlocks one Codex line.
- **Firstborn encounters:** rare ship-borne meetings, each with a different "account" of the Rending that contradicts the others.
- **Soumaya:** one or two lines, ever.
- **Never:** punish the player; erase their data; make the Hush a timer.
