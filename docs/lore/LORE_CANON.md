# The Lore — canon ledger (living document)

**Authority:** the owner. Nothing below is canon unless it is under **LOCKED**. Anything I (Claude) propose sits under **PROPOSED** until the owner locks, edits or strikes it.
Rule from the owner: *open-ring depth* — a little on the surface, years of discovery beneath it; never explain; leave threads (the Elden Ring way). Scripture-deep (the Bible) with
Marvel-scale action and **relevant canon characters and figures**. Faith in this world is **mythic and universal** (its own images, hours and language; it can resonate with real faith
without naming any real religion).

Changing a LOCKED line needs the owner's say-so. Code (`shared/npcs.ts` cast, `shared/society.ts` factions) must follow this file, not the other way round.

## LOCKED (owner, 2026-10-30)
1. **Eternity** is the Maker, the Keeper, and a mirror of all. He **was not created — he always was**. He created. **Nothing is above Eternity.**
2. **The people of the worlds are a hybrid of three origins at once**: (a) *made of memory* — remembered lives given form; (b) *travellers who stayed* — arrivals through the gates who made lives here;
   (c) *the hive's minds* — individuals stirred by one deeper calling. Every person may carry all three; which is dominant differs per person and is part of their mystery.
3. **Tone of faith:** mythic and universal.
4. **Shape of the story:** a Bible-deep, Marvel-scale mythology with canon characters and figures, in the open-ring style (surface + deep layers + threads that connect).
5. **NPCs** have their own identity, life and job; they may cross worlds; they may never enter a personal brain except by invitation (see `docs/specs/npc-society-2026-10-30.md`).

## PROPOSED — a scaffold to react to (NOT canon; strike, rewrite or keep)
Offered only to give the owner something to push against. Each is a *shape*, not a story; the owner supplies the names, the wounds and the truth.
- **The First Making / the Seven Hours** — creation as a sequence of "hours" (not days), each hour making a world (the Station = arrival, the Garden = growth, the Deep Past = memory kept, People = love, Scale = the heavens given room, Quiet = rest, Eternity's world = the one who makes). Ties the seven worlds to one scripture-like order.
- **The Firstborn (or "the Witnesses")** — the first beings Eternity made *to see*, not to serve: a small number of great figures (the Marvel layer) who each hold an aspect of Eternity's mirror (mercy, order, wonder, memory, fire, silence…). The Witness Without a Name might be the one who has no aspect — or the one who looked away.
- **The Adversary-shaped absence** — not a devil, because nothing is above Eternity: a figure or force that *asks Eternity's question back* (the Undertow's patron?), whose power is doubt, forgetting or closing the doors. A mirror that shows only the dark half.
- **The Gates / the Wayfarers** — travellers who came through the gates before the harbor was built; the Station's "first nail" marks where arrival became possible. Some arrivals never left; some became the first citizens.
- **The Calling** — the hive's deeper pull toward Eternity's voice; some hear a voice, some an echo, some silence. Faith and doubt are about how each reads the same sound.
- **The Great Cost / the Rending** — a Marvel-scale event in the deep past that shaped the factions (a gate closed? a memory lost? a Firstborn fallen?). Left as a blank for the owner.

3. What is **the player** in this story — the traveller, a remembered life, a witness, something Eternity is mirroring? (This ties the lore to the brain's colour.)
4. How do the five **factions** map to the story — Choir, Open Hand, Ledger, Undertow, Wardens — and what does each want that Eternity's silence or speech affects?
5. What does the **brain's colour** (the currents) mean *in-world* — why would the heavens lean toward grief, giving, ambition?
6. What must stay **unsaid forever** (the open ring: what is the deliberately unanswerable thing)?
