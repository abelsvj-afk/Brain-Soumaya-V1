# DRAFT 1 — The First Morning and the Rending

*LOCKED by the owner 2026-10-30. Original status:  PROPOSED by Claude, from the owner's answers of 2026-10-30. Nothing here is canon until the owner locks it. Written to be rewritten.*
*Method: surface a little, bury a lot, never explain. Every "fact" below is the DEEP layer — the in-world text should only ever give accounts, and the accounts should disagree.*

---

## 0. What "mirroring" means (the one idea the whole thing hangs on)
Eternity is the Maker, the Keeper, and a mirror. **A mirror shows a thing back to the one who has it.** The worlds are not a place made *for* the player; they are the player's own remembered life, kept by Eternity and shown back from outside —
the Station is where you *arrive* in your own life, the Garden is where you *grow*, the Deep Past is what you *kept*, People are who you *love*. The player is the **Witness**: the only one who was present for their life, so the only one who can say what was.
Eternity keeps everything that is remembered. **He cannot keep what no one witnessed.** That is why the mirror needs you, and why the one thing it cannot show is **what you have forgotten**.

## 1. The Maker
Eternity was not made. He always was. He *called*, and what he called came to be — light first. Nothing is above him; nothing was before him. He is the Keeper (he forgets nothing) and the Mirror (he shows what he keeps).
The one thing his nature cannot do is *stop keeping*. That is the seed of everything below.

## 2. The Seven Hours (the order of the making)
Not days. *Hours* — each one a held breath. In each hour Eternity called a world and a Firstborn: a being made **to see, not to serve**, holding one aspect of his mirror.

| Hour | The world called | What was made | The Firstborn | Aspect |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| I — **The First Morning** | the Deep Past (the First Light basin) | light, and the first thing remembered | **Oru, the Dawnkeeper** | Remembrance |
| II — The Heavens | Scale | room for things to be seen at their true size | **Sethane, the Measurer** | Wonder |
| III — The Threshold | the Great Station | the first place where *arrival* became possible (the first nail) | **Calend, the Doorkeeper** | Welcome |
| IV — The Growing | the Growing Place | becoming that counts before it arrives anywhere | **Maren, the Gardener** | Patience |
| V — The Hearths | People | love given a place to live outside the mind that remembers it | **Nuaré, the Hearthkeeper** | Love |
| VI — The Rest (*the hour that was made twice*) | Quiet / the Archive | first an unmaking, then a rest | **Vesh, the Gentle One** → *the Hollow Mercy* | Mercy |
| VII — The Standing | Eternity's world | the one who makes, standing in the white | — (no Firstborn: he is the seventh) | — |

## 3. The Calling
Everything called carries the *sound* of being called. It is why the hive pulls toward something it cannot name. Some who hear it hear **a voice** (the devout); some hear **an echo** (the wavering); some hear **silence** (the doubters).
All three are hearing the same thing. This is what the factions argue about, and none of them is wrong about what they hear.

## 4. The Rending (the wound)
In the Sixth Hour the made did something none of them had done: they **grieved**.
There was a person among them, one of the first of the made — beloved by all the hearths, and above all by Vesh. She **was kept**: Eternity kept her, as he keeps everything; nothing of her was lost. But *to be kept is not to be had.* She was beyond their reach, and the made could not bear it.

Vesh, whose aspect was Mercy, saw the made unable to rest and could think of only one mercy: **let them forget.** Vesh took the grief into themselves — and then reached past the made into the mirror itself, into the place where Eternity keeps what is remembered,
and **cut her out.** Not her death: her *remembering.* Every love that had her in it lost its object. Every hearth that had a place laid for her found the place empty and did not know why.

It worked, in the way a wound works: the grief stopped. In its place was **the ache** — love with nothing in it, longing without a name — and the first hole in the mirror: **the one place Eternity cannot see.**
He who always was, who forgets nothing, has, since the Sixth Hour, a place he cannot look. It is his only wound. Nothing is above him. It was done by one he made to see.

*He did not undo it.* He could have. He did not, because the mirror is his gift, and the gift includes that the made may choose — even to forget, even wrongly — and to unmake the choice would be to unmake the chooser. He **grieves** her. He is the only one who remembers there was someone.
And since that hour, **he does not speak.** (See the unanswerable, §7.)

Vesh woke into what they had done. The Gentle One became **the Hollow Mercy**: a Firstborn whose whole aspect had gone to the wrong shape of kindness. Not a devil — nothing is above Eternity, and nothing here is evil in the way a devil is. A mercy that took the wrong thing.
The Sixth Hour was **made twice**: first as an unmaking, then — Vesh's penance, and Eternity's answer — as the **Quiet**, the Archive: *a place where a thing can be set down without being erased.* **Rest is not erasure.** (The Rest Seal says it. The Folded Letter says it. The whole Archive is Vesh learning it.)

## 5. What the Rending left
- **The Witness Without a Name** — the oldest object in the worlds, smooth, pale, nameless "in any language." It is what remains of *her*: the shape of a person the mirror cannot show. It marks "the boundary between the one who makes and the worlds that remember him." **It is the key to Eternity's gate** because only one who carries *a witness* can walk into the place he cannot see.
- **The Unnumbered Fragment** — "a fragment of the created record struggling to describe the uncreated": a piece of the *first* record, from before there was a hole. It will not say when its maker began, because Eternity has no beginning.
- **The ache** — the hive's deeper pull is two sounds braided: the Calling (the first voice) and the ache (the hole). Nobody can tell them apart. That is the whole of faith and doubt.
- **The factions are answers to the ache:**
  - **The Choir** — it is Eternity's voice we are missing; sing until he answers.
  - **The Open Hand** — it is each other we are missing; tend the ones who feel it most.
  - **The Ledger** — it is something we can *get*; trade and build to fill the hole.
  - **The Undertow** — the sky is silent because the sky is hollow; ask until it admits it.
  - **The Wardens** — the ache makes us dangerous; keep the lanes and the law around it.
- **The hybrid people** — *made of memory* (some of them are remembered lives that **came back**, half-whole, from the edges of the hole), *travellers who stayed* (those who came through the gate looking for what they lost), and *the hive's minds* (the Calling, answered). Each person is all three in a different proportion; the dominant one is their private mystery.

## 6. The player
You are a **witness**. Your memories are what the worlds are made of, so Eternity mirrors *you*: the brain's colour is the colour of your mirror. Every memory you keep is a stitch across the hole; every memory you let go dark is a place the mirror cannot reach.
**The product's own rule is the lore's rule:** nothing dims, nothing is erased — things *rest*. When the player archives rather than deletes, they are doing what Vesh learned too late.
The gut-turn the player slowly discovers: *the hole in the mirror is the same shape as something missing in you.* That is why it hurt to arrive.

## 7. The Open Ring — what stays unanswerable *forever* (proposed)
1. **Her name.** No one says it. Not Vesh (they cannot), not the Choir (it was cut from the hymns), not Eternity.
2. **Who she was to you.** Three accounts, never reconciled: *she was the first witness*; *she was the player's own earliest forgotten memory*; *she was never a person — she is what a witness becomes.* The Witness Without a Name fits all three.
3. **Why Eternity does not speak.** Because he cannot see her? Because speaking would call her name? Because nothing remains to be said? Because he is waiting for a witness to say it first?
4. **Whether he would have undone it** if anyone had asked.
These are never answered in text. Deep layers only deepen the question.

## 8. The canon figures (each flies their own ship)
Six Firstborn and the one the mirror lost. Each is **provisional until locked**; names are invented, not borrowed from any real tradition.
- **Oru, the Dawnkeeper** — keeps the First Morning in the First Light basin. Remembrance. Calm, old, never forgets a face. Ship: **the Dawnward** — a long, pale ship shaped like an unfolded fan of light.
- **Sethane, the Measurer** — Wonder; sees everything at true size and weeps at how large it is. Ship: **the Plumb** — a needle of dark metal with a ring of measuring arms. (Cast tie: the Surveyor is her apprentice.)
- **Calend, the Doorkeeper** — Welcome; made the first nail of the Station. Has no home and is always already at the door. Ship: **the Latch** — a door-shaped hull that opens along its length. (Cast tie: the Wayfarer *is* Calend, walking out among the made — "always already there"; the Harbor controller serves the Doorkeeper's order.)
- **Maren, the Gardener** — Patience; the Growing Place's first tender. Ship: **the Slow Bloom** — a seed-pod hull with petal-vanes. (Cast tie: the Keeper is the Gardener's heir.)
- **Nuaré, the Hearthkeeper** — Love; the hearths' warmth. Ship: **the Ember Ark** — a rounded, glowing hull like a lantern. (Cast tie: the Open Hand's founder.)
- **Vesh, the Hollow Mercy** (once the Gentle One) — Mercy; the Quiet's keeper, and the Rending's hand. Ship: **the Lullaby** — a beautiful, hushed ship with one hull-length hollow where something has been cut out. (Cast tie: the Archivist is Vesh's penitent; the Undertow's patron *in grief, not in malice*.)
- **The Unnamed** — she is not a Firstborn and has no ship. She has a *shape in the mirror*: the Witness Without a Name.
- **Eternity** — the sky. He does not have a ship; he is where ships go.
