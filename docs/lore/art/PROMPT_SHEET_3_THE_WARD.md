# Lore art — Prompt Sheet 3 (the Faded Ward): the new images

*From Claude, 2026-10-31, for Muse. The Faded Ward (seam S-VII) is built as a world: one long hall of narrow cots, ember lanterns down its length, the far end lost in fog. Twelve new Book entries live there. Same rules as Sheets 1–2: read `docs/lore/CONTINUITY_BIBLE.md` first; one prompt per image, 4 variants each (v1–v4), filed `NN-short-title-vK.png` in `docs/lore/art/`; numbering continues from §73; report in `IMAGE_QA_NOTES.md`, flag story questions, don't rule on them. I pick one by eye and place it in the Book.*

**Global style line (append verbatim):** mythic space-fantasy concept art, painterly cinematic realism, soft volumetric light, deep indigo-black space with warm gold and pale cyan accents, restrained palette, heavy atmosphere, large sense of scale, no text, no logos, no UI, no watermarks, no real-world religious symbols.

**Never (this sheet):** no corpses, no graves, no gore, nothing that reads as a morgue — the ward is **resting**, never dead. **No count of cots or patients anywhere** (the hall always runs into fog). The sleepers are **half-transparent, watercolour-soft** shapes, **never a clear face**. **No readable writing** — every cot tag is blank. Tamsin: middle-aged, greying brown hair in a bun, rolled-sleeve green apron (face reference §23 v1/§47). Hesk: big, stern, grey-bearded, Warden's coat (face reference §45). Ages stay fixed per the Bible.

## NEW PROMPTS

### §73 — The Door Left Ajar *(sight.ward-door)*
Aspect: 16:9. A plain stone arch at the end of a very long hall, the door standing a hand's breadth open; a thin blade of pale light lies across the threshold and out into the dark. Beyond it, rows of narrow cots recede into soft fog. Nobody visible. Mood: a welcome that does not want to wake anyone. Palette: pale green, ember gold, soft grey-blue.

### §74 — The Hall That Goes On *(sight.ward-long-hall)*
Aspect: 21:9 panorama. A vast, long ward seen from the door: two walls of narrow cots, made up and tidy, receding past sight into a lit fog; open ribbed roof with stars between the ribs; ember lanterns hung evenly down the middle. Tiny, far off, a single green-aproned figure walking the rows. Mood: patient, endless, kind. Palette: pale green, ember gold, indigo.

### §75 — The Lanterns Trimmed by Night *(sight.ward-lanterns)*
Aspect: 16:9. Close on one hung ember lantern in the ward at night, a pair of hands in a rolled-sleeve cuff (face and body out of frame, only hands and forearm) turning the wick to exactly the same height as the one beside it; a long row of identical lanterns glows away behind. Mood: unseen care. Palette: ember gold, pale green, dusk.

### §76 — The Bowl That Stays Warm *(obj.ward-bowl)*
Aspect: 3:2. On a small round table in the aisle of the ward, one white bowl of soup with steam rising and curling into the lantern-light; sleeping cots on both sides, soft and blurred. Nobody beside it. Mood: a kindness left where it will be found. Palette: warm white, ember gold, pale green.

### §77 — The Soldier Beside the Small Bed *(obj.ward-soldier)*
Aspect: 16:9. A small child's bed, made, with a dent in the pillow; on the floor beside it a worn tin soldier standing guard, facing the distant door, its red paint rubbed away where a thumb held it. A faint, watercolour outline of a child's silhouette in the bedclothes — a memory, no face. Mood: a guard kept for someone small. Palette: rust red, pale green, ember gold.

### §78 — The Window That Keeps One Night *(sight.ward-window)*
Aspect: 16:9. A tall arched window in the wall of the ward showing the same few stars; the glass wiped clean from the inside (a cloth folded on the sill). Cots in soft focus below, lantern-light on the floor. Mood: the same night, kept on purpose. Palette: deep blue, pale gold, pale green.

### §79 — A Button in a Pocket *(obj.ward-button)*
Aspect: 3:2. Tamsin's open hand in the lantern-light holding five or six loose buttons of different sizes and metals; her green apron pocket beside it; a sleeping patient blurred behind her shoulder. Face partly turned, tender, not looking at the camera. Mood: things kept for someone who may return. Palette: pale green, brass, ember gold.

### §80 — The Cot with a Blank Tag *(sight.ward-blank-cot)*
Aspect: 16:9. One cot freshly made with a folded blanket and a tag at its foot with nothing written on it; above it on the wall, a long plain coat on a hook beneath a single small lamp. The neighbouring cots hold faint sleepers; this one is smooth, expectant. Mood: one more than the count. Palette: pale green, ember gold, soft grey.

### §81 — The Hum Over the Cots *(echo.ward-hum)*
Aspect: 16:9. A narrow cot in the ward from which a soft, visible hum rises as gold thread-lines curling up through the lantern-light and out toward the roof ribs; the sleeper is only a faint outline, no face. Other cots hush around it. Mood: the same pitch as a laugh that came before. Palette: gold, pale green, indigo.

### §82 — A Breath From the Far End *(echo.ward-far-breath)*
Aspect: 21:9. The far end of the hall where the fog thickens into a pale glow; a slow wave of lantern-light brightens and dims along the whole hall in one long breath, in and out. A small figure stands facing it, seen from behind. Mood: something resting, keeping time with the lanterns. Palette: pale blue-white, ember gold, indigo.

### §83 — The Count She Loses *(echo.ward-count)*
Aspect: 16:9. Tamsin walking the rows at night, lips moving, one hand trailing a finger along the foot-rails of the cots as she counts; behind her the rows continue into fog; a small smile as she begins again. Face allowed, three-quarter. Mood: patient, unbothered, always one more. Palette: pale green, ember gold, soft grey.

### §84 — What Is Left by the Door *(obj.ward-wake)*
Aspect: 3:2. On the stone sill beside the ajar ward door, a single polished tin soldier standing upright in the night; behind it Hesk's broad back in his brown Warden's coat, walking away down the corridor, a ring of small keys at his belt. Face never shown. Mood: a guard left, quietly, by someone who won't say why. Palette: rust red, brown, ember gold, indigo.
