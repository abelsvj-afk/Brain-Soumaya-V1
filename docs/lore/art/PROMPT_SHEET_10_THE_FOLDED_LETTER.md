# Prompt Sheet 10 — The Folded Letter (§§145–154), for Muse
Same style, identity cards and never-list as Sheets 2–9. Four variants each. **No figure and no face; no readable text on any paper (not even a hint of handwriting); nothing says whether the sentence is kind.**

145. **An Empty Room, a Letter on the Floor** — a small bare room open to the night, one letter folded in thirds on the floor.
146. **Still Folded** — close on the letter: a soft, much-handled fold, unopened.
147. **A Place for the Reader** — an empty cushion in a pool of lamplight facing the letter, exactly the size of whoever comes.
148. **A Chair Drawn Back** — a plain chair drawn back from a small table; a capped pen, no paper.
149. **A Shelf of Kept Letters** — a long dusted shelf of folded letters, each with one thumb's press on its back, no two alike.
150. **A Lamp Left Lit** — a low lamp beside the letter, oil freshly topped, nobody there.
151. **A Door Left Ajar** — a door open by one hand's breadth, night beyond.
152. **A Seat Worn by Long Sitting** — a low seat by the door, smooth from years of use.
153. **The Sound of a Fold Pressed** — a hand's shadow folding a letter again along an old crease (shadow only; no face).
154. **A Disc of Smooth Wax** — a smooth unmarked seal-wax disc held in a gloved hand (hand only).
