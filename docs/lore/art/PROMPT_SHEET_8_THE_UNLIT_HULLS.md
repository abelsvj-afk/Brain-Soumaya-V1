# Prompt Sheet 8 — The Unlit Hulls (§§125–134), for Muse
Same style, identity cards and never-list as Sheets 2–7. Four variants each. **No figure appears; no readable text or letters anywhere (plates carry strokes only); nothing is lit; never the name.**

125. **Six Hulls, Each an Answer** — a vast dim shipyard, six long unlit hulls on slips, each finished in one part only (dawn-prow, plumb keel, latch fin, bloom wing, ember engine, lullaby flank).
126. **A Seventh Slip, Ribs Only** — a lone slip with a frame of ribs and no plating, built by many hands, echoing the six.
127. **Six Plates on a Rail** — six brass nameplates cut with slow, even strokes, long and short, no letters.
128. **A Plate Left Smooth** — a seventh brass plate on a bench, bright from handling, never cut; a graver laid down square.
129. **A Desk With No Ledger** — a clerk's desk facing the seventh slip; a ring on the wood where a book sat; a trimmed pen; nothing written.
130. **Tools Laid Down Square** — rows of shipwrights' tools, no two grips alike.
131. **Lanterns Trimmed and Unlit** — a rack of yard lanterns, filled, none lit.
132. **A Crane With an Empty Hook** — a tall crane over the seventh slip, the hook at the height of a flank.
133. **One Mallet, Far Off** — an empty yard in the stillest hour, a single mallet resting on a beam, mid-stroke.
134. **A Curl of Bright Metal** — a thin curl of brass held in a clerk's hand (hand only), no letters in it.
