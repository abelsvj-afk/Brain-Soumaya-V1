# Prompt Sheet 6 — The Unmade Hour (§§105–114), for Muse
Same style, identity cards and never-list as Sheets 2–5. Four variants each; Claude picks and places. **No figure appears in any picture; no text; nothing explains why.**

105. **Hollows That Lead Nowhere** — a wide pale lilac-grey floor, shallow footstep-sized hollows in converging rows leaning toward a thin ring; nothing in them; dusk light.
106. **The Ring the Steps Stop At** — close on the ring line: hollows come up to it from every side and none cross; inside, untouched smooth floor.
107. **A Place Worn by Standing** — a patch worn pale and smooth, two feet wide, a small unlit lamp wiped clean at its edge; no one there.
108. **Seven Open Hands** — seven grey stone hands in a circle, palms up; six hold a small steady warm light, the seventh holds the same hollow and nothing.
109. **A Frame With Nothing In It** — a tall mirror-sized frame, no glass, the same floor going on a little darker through it.
110. **Marks Cut in Groups** — a low curved wall of finger-wide strokes in groups, each closed by a short crossing line; the last group left open.
111. **A Step One Hand Thick** — a clean step across a pale floor, one hand high; the grain of the floor beyond runs the other way.
112. **A Note Held and Cut** — a column of faint light with no source, thinnest at the top, in a still pale hall.
113. **A Floor That Keeps No Marks** — a ship's shadow crossing a floor that shows no track behind it.
114. **A Leaf Set Down, Not Burned** — a soft-edged blank leaf of paper held in the Archivist's gloved hands (hands only, no face).
