## relic.witness-without-a-name — The Witness Without a Name
Wide 16:9 composition. In the lowest stratum of a vast ancient basin, where pale first light pools on still water, a single smooth, plain, opal-white object rests at the water's edge on dark stone — small, unadorned, perfect, glowing very faintly from within. No figure stands here; the place itself feels watched-over and silent, as if the object were the only form someone left behind. Towering layered rock walls rise into darkness around the basin.

## relic.witness-seed — The Witness Seed
Wide 16:9 composition. At the base of an immense ancient tree in a green terraced growing place, among great roots and soft moss, rests a single seed-shaped relic the size of a held stone — bare, matte, unglowing, patient. Dawn light filters through leaves above; seedlings and small green shoots crowd the soil around it, but the seed itself simply rests, holding the possibility of becoming and nothing more.

## relic.first-light-shard — The First Light Shard
Wide 16:9 composition. Near the rim of a great pale basin in a deep ancient stratum, a single translucent shard rests on dark rock — a fragment like frozen light, warm-white radiance held quietly inside it, see-through at its edges. The basin's still surface below mirrors its glow. Nothing else competes with it; the whole scene is the difference between the light itself and the trace of it.

## relic.basin-stone — The Basin Stone
Wide 16:9 composition. On the floor of a vast empty basin, far below layered rock walls, one dark stone rests alone — its surface marked with shallow horizontal lines like the waterlines of forgotten waters. A thin skin of still water nearby reflects a pale sky. The stone is bare and unglowing, kept rather than lost; the scale of the empty basin makes it feel deliberately placed.

## relic.lantern-of-keeping — The Lantern of Keeping
Wide 16:9 composition. In a low stone alcove cut into the lowest stratum, above a great dark basin, a small sealed lantern burns with a quiet warm light — not bright, but present, the kind of light that makes something near without forcing it into view. Scrolls and plain vessels rest in neighbouring alcoves. Far below, the basin's surface catches one soft reflection.

## relic.hearth-ember — The Hearth Ember
Wide 16:9 composition. Beside the stones of one of the first hearths in a warm, low-roofed dwelling place, a single coal-like fragment rests in old ash — unburning and unconsumed, a low amber glow breathing in its cracks. Cushions and plain clay vessels sit near the hearth; the room feels loved and lived-in, and the ember is its kept heart.

## relic.sunward-compass — The Sunward Compass
Wide 16:9 composition. Along a vast bright road of light in open scale-space, a round instrument of bare metal rests on a dark stone plinth — no needle, its centre fixed and calm while fine markings around its rim suggest slow motion. It points nowhere in particular. Enormous distances fall away on every side; a distant sun gilds one edge of the metal.

## relic.unopened-fold — The Unopened Fold
Wide 16:9 composition. In a hushed archive of tall shelves and scrolls, one small folded object rests on a middle shelf in a pool of soft lamplight — folded like paper without quite being paper, its edges neat, its seal unbroken, deliberately unopened. Everything about the scene is permission: it is kept, and keeping is enough. Dust motes drift in the light; nothing reaches for it.

## relic.unnumbered-fragment — The Unnumbered Fragment
Wide 16:9 composition. In a vast uncounted expanse of dark scale-space, a small translucent fragment turns slowly, a pale glow held inside it. Fine inscription-like markings cover one face, worn past all reading — no date, no age, no beginning can be made out. Distant unnumbered lights surround it; no world claims it, and it rests content to be unclaimed.

## sight.dead-bough — The Dead Bough
Wide 16:9 composition. A colossal ancient tree crowns a terraced growing place, its canopy vast and green — except one great bough that has never leafed, bare and pale, pointing out over an unlooked direction. Tiny travellers pass far below on a path; at the very tip of the bare bough, a few small new leaves are just greening. Dawn light, dew, long shadows.

## sight.open-alcove — The Open Alcove
Wide 16:9 composition. Along a quiet archive wall, rows of sealed alcoves stand shut and labelled with blank tags; one alcove stands open and empty, and inside it, alone, lies a folded letter that has never been unfolded. Before it, seen from behind, a single still figure stands in the night-quiet, keeping the length of a lullaby. Lamplight is low; the open alcove is the only lit doorway in the wall.

## obj.tin-soldier — Hesk's Tin Soldier

Behind, out of focus, shelves of small kept belongings with blank paper tags. The soldier faces outward, on guard over the room — treasured, not discarded.

## sight.second-seam — The Second Seam
Wide 16:9 composition. The lower rim of an immense harbour structure at night: a seam older than any nail runs along the great curved metal, never opened, and some nights a thin line of white lies along it like held breath. Tiny dock lamps and a few distant workers give the scale. The seam hums below hearing; the white line is the brightest thing in the frame and leads the eye out of it.

## obj.faded-ticket — The Faded Ticket
Wide 16:9 composition. On a scarred wooden table in harbour lamplight, a single old voyage ticket lies face-up — its printed date smudged soft by a thumb that turned it over a thousand times, none of its marks readable. A folded coat and a cold cup rest nearby, out of focus. The ticket is the warm centre of the scene: a voyage wanted, and never forgotten on purpose.

## obj.vesh-paper-boat — Vesh's Paper Boat
Wide 16:9 composition. A little boat folded from a page of archive paper rests on the edge of a stone basin of still water, dusk light coming through it: on its inside fold, the first single stroke of a name that was never finished, too faint to read. Soft violet-grey cloth — the edge of a hooded robe — is just visible out of focus at the frame's edge, already turning away. Gentle, merciful, unhurried.

## obj.doorkeepers-nail — The Doorkeeper's Nail
Wide 16:9 composition. An old travel-worn hand, palm open, rests on the frame of a tall door standing on a night harbour pier; above the palm hovers not a nail but the shape of one — a nail-shaped warmth, a faint amber shimmer in the air with nothing inside it, always warm. The door stands one hand's-breadth open, light along its edge. The shape opens nothing, and the way on feels a little easier.

## sight.empty-chair — The Empty Chair
Wide 16:9 composition. Inside a modest lamplit home at dusk, a family table is laid for one more than the household holds: plates, cups, and one chair drawn up to a place that is set and empty. No one is in the room; the other chairs are pushed in, the empty one is not. Warm light, a window on the evening harbour — the place is kept, not mourned.

## obj.dissenters-chart — The Dissenter's Chart
Wide 16:9 composition. On a chart-table in a draughtsman's lamplight, a great chart is unrolled: drawn with a clean edge and no coastline, it shows one perfect smooth circle — the chart of the one place no one has charted. Around the circle, the margin carries angry strokes of handwriting, all worn illegible, the last line struck through. A hand rests flat at the chart's edge, stilling it. No face is shown.

## sight.unlooked-direction — The Direction He Does Not Look
Wide 16:9 composition. Seen from behind and far below, a vast radiant male presence in white-on-white stands over a field of stars, a thin ring of his own light behind his head, his broad gaze sweeping everything — except one quiet direction: a darker, still wedge of sky at the frame's edge where his attention never falls, beside a tiny hooded figure seen from behind. Nothing is visible in that direction, and that is the point of it. No face is shown.

## sight.ward-door — The Door Left Ajar
Wide 16:9 composition. The door of a quiet ward of narrow cots stands ajar — it has no lock and has never been shut all the way — and a hand's breadth of the hall's warm light lies across the threshold at night. A half-transparent, watercolour-soft figure slips through the gap carefully, not widening it. Inside, rows of cots rest under trimmed lanterns; everything is hushed and kept.

## sight.ward-long-hall — The Hall That Goes On
Wide 16:9 composition. A long ward hall: made-up cots stand tidy along both walls and run on into pale fog at the far end, farther than the hall should reach. The near cots are crisp and plain; the far ones soften into mist without ever ending. Lanterns hang at even heights. Nobody in the hall seems to want to say where the cots stop.

## sight.ward-lanterns — The Lanterns Trimmed by Night
Wide 16:9 composition. Down a quiet ward hall, every hanging lantern burns at exactly the same height and the same even flame, wicks never short, trimmed in the night by someone no visitor has met. Half-transparent resting figures lie on the cots below in watercolour softness. The sameness of the flames is the subject: care, repeated perfectly, by unseen hands.

## obj.ward-bowl — The Bowl That Stays Warm
Wide 16:9 composition. In the aisle of a quiet ward, on a small plain table between the cots, a single bowl of soup gives off a thin curl of steam — warm whatever the hour, never seen emptied or refilled. Lantern light pools on the table; the cots on either side rest in soft shadow, their occupants half-transparent and watercolour-pale.

## obj.ward-soldier — The Soldier Beside the Small Bed
Wide 16:9 composition. On the floor beside a small made bed in the ward stands a tin soldier, polished until its paint has worn through, facing the door. The small bed's pillow carries a child's dent. A cot-lantern burns low nearby. The soldier is on guard and the bed is kept — nothing in the scene is sad in the way of loss; it is watched over.

## sight.ward-window — The Window That Keeps One Night
Wide 16:9 composition. A tall window in the ward's left wall shows the same few stars in the same places, at an hour the hall's lanterns say is deep night — the window keeps one night and does not change it. The glass is wiped clean from the inside, one clear arc in a faint misting. Below it, cots rest in rows under warm lantern light.

## obj.ward-button — A Button in a Pocket
Wide 16:9 composition. Close on a healer's rolled-sleeve green apron as she walks the ward rows: one weathered hand turns loose buttons over in the apron pocket, a handful of them, every one from a different coat. She is middle-aged, kind, mid-conversation with someone off-frame. Behind her, softly out of focus, the cots and lanterns of the ward go on.

## sight.ward-blank-cot — The Cot with a Blank Tag
Wide 16:9 composition. Among the ward's occupied cots, one cot is made up fresh: blanket folded, pillow smooth, and at its foot a paper tag with nothing written on it. On the hook above hangs a plain coat that belongs to no one in the ward. Morning lantern light; the cot is ready, and whoever made it up does not say who it is for.

## echo.ward-hum — The Hum Over the Cots
Wide 16:9 composition. Night in the ward: a middle-aged healer in a green apron stands still in the aisle, head tilted, listening. Under the quiet, one cot among the rows seems to be the source of a low, even hum — the lantern flames nearest it lean almost imperceptibly toward it. The resting figures are half-transparent and peaceful; the hum belongs to no one in the hall.

## echo.ward-far-breath — A Breath From the Far End
Wide 16:9 composition. At the far end of the ward hall, where the fog thickens between the last cots, the mist itself breathes — swelling gently out and settling back, slow and even, in time with the lanterns' glow, which rises and dims with it. It is not any one sleeper's breath. The near hall is still and lamplit; the far end keeps its own slow time.

## echo.ward-count — The Count She Loses
Wide 16:9 composition. A middle-aged healer in a rolled-sleeve green apron walks the ward rows at night, one hand raised mid-count, lips just moving, passing the same cot where she always loses the count — and beginning again without complaint, unhurried, fond. Lanterns at even heights; half-transparent patients rest on the cots; there is always one more than she reaches.

## obj.ward-wake — What Is Left by the Door
Wide 16:9 composition. Night at the ward's door: on the sill beside it, left overnight, stands a small polished tin soldier, bright in the last lantern light, on guard over the threshold. The hall behind is dark and resting. By morning it will have been taken away again, and the warden who leaves it will not say where it goes — but tonight it stands.

## sight.road-mouth — Where the Road Leaves the Station
Wide 16:9 composition. Under a great harbour station, where the last dock lamp gives out, a plain stone arch opens in the wall onto a road that goes down into quiet dark. No sign names it; no berth lists it. A few dockhands cross the harbour floor toward the arch on an errand, and the ones coming back walk a little quieter. Harbour light behind, road-dark ahead.

## sight.road-lamps — Lamps at Even Spacing
Wide 16:9 composition. A road under the world: lamps hang at the same height and the same distance along both walls, and they are lit a little ahead of wherever the lone traveller stands — the lamps just before him bright, the ones behind already dimming, the far ones dark. He is small in the frame, hooded, seen from behind. No one has seen who lights them.

## obj.road-nails — A Nail at Every Stride
Wide 16:9 composition. Along the right-hand wall of an underground road, at the height of a man's shoulder, an iron nail is set at every stride — each one faintly warm, a soft amber shimmer at its head as if just set — and the line of them runs straighter than anything else on the road, stride after stride, into lamplit distance.

## sight.road-chalk — Chalk Low on the Wall
Wide 16:9 composition. Low on the left-hand wall of the road, at the height of a child, runs a line of short chalk strokes counted off in small groups — never smudged, going on for a very long way beside the empty road. A single lamp's light rakes across them. Whoever made them walked beside this wall for a long way and did not once stop to rest; they are not here now.

## obj.road-doorframe — A Door-Frame With Nothing In It
Wide 16:9 composition. Against the right-hand wall of the underground road stands a door-frame with no door in it and nothing behind it but more wall. Its hinges are bright and freshly oiled. Lamplight catches the polished hinge metal; the road passes it by. Someone keeps those hinges oiled, and has never been seen to use them.

## sight.road-post — The Wardens' Post
Wide 16:9 composition. At the roadside under the world stands an old iron post, and beside it a bench worn pale in exactly two places where sitters have sat. The ground around it is swept clean. No warden is stationed here, and none has been for longer than the harbour keeps records — but the bench is swept, and the post stands its watch alone in the lamplight.

## sight.road-chalk-end — Where the Chalk Stops
Wide 16:9 composition. Low on the road's left wall, the long line of chalk strokes stops — in the middle of a group, one short stroke half-finished, as though the one making them had been called away. Past that point the wall is bare and the road goes on exactly as it did before: same lamps, same stone, same quiet.

## echo.road-steps — Steps Ahead
Wide 16:9 composition. A lone hooded traveller stands perfectly still in the middle of the underground road, head raised, listening: ahead of him the lamplit road is empty — and yet the whole scene leans toward a presence just out of the light, footsteps that keep their distance exactly, small and sure. The lamps ahead burn steady; nothing is there, and something is.

## obj.road-oil — A Can of Oil for the Lamps
Wide 16:9 composition. By the side of the underground road, set neatly against the wall, stands a small can of lamp oil — lid screwed down, sides dry, not a drop spilled — left exactly where it will be needed, by someone who does not say by whom. Lamplight from the road's even lamps catches its plain metal shoulder.

## sight.road-far — The Road Goes On
Wide 16:9 composition. Past the road's last lamp, the floor goes on level into fog — and the fog is lit from within, a soft glow with no source and no end. Nothing here suggests an ending, and nothing suggests anyone is afraid of that. The last lamp stands at the frame's edge behind; ahead, only the lit fog and the level road, going on.

## sight.village-gate — The Gate Where a Count Was Kept
Wide 16:9 composition. Across a village gate at dusk lies a heavy wooden bar cut with notches — one for each who went through — and the bar has been recut more than once, so the rows of notches do not agree with one another. Amber window-light falls across the gate from the houses beyond. Nobody will say which cut is the true one.

## sight.village-windows — Every Window Lit
Wide 16:9 composition. A village street at dusk: every house has its windows lit with the amber of an evening meal, and no one is seen at any of them. The lights have been that amber at every hour a visitor has come. Lamps unlit, street empty, the whole village warm and window-lit and quietly unpeopled.

## obj.village-well — The Well With Two Ropes
Wide 16:9 composition. In a village square at dusk, a stone well carries two ropes over its beam and a single bucket. One rope is worn smooth and dark with use; the other is nearly new, pale, and has never been seen to be used. Amber light from the square's hearth and windows; the well is the still centre of the village.

## sight.village-chair — A Chair Set Outside One Door
Wide 16:9 composition. At first light, outside one village door, a plain chair is set in the street turned to face the path that climbs the hill. It has been dusted this morning; a cloth rests over its back. Whoever sits in it is gone before the village wakes. The hill path rises behind it, empty, into kind morning light.

## sight.village-hearth — A Hearth Laid for the Whole Village
Wide 16:9 composition. In the village square, a great hearth is laid with more stones than a hearth needs and a ring of plain seats around it. The seats are warm — a faint life in the fresh ash, a low ember glow — and no one is ever sitting in them. Dusk; the houses' windows lit amber around the square.

## obj.village-hooks — A Hook for Every Hand
Wide 16:9 composition. Along the path that climbs the hill out of the village, small gold hooks are set one stride apart, each at the height of a held hand, catching the light as the line of them rises toward a doorway at the top. Partway up, the line bends a little, where something once pulled it taut. Morning; the village below is quiet.

## sight.village-door — The Doorway at the Path's End
Wide 16:9 composition. At the top of the hill path stands a tall doorway of pale light. It shows nothing through it; it is very bright, and it is kind. Far down the path, a small hooded figure seen from behind has stopped at a respectful distance — not asked to go through, and not afraid. Gold hooks glint along the path behind them.

## obj.village-bread — Bread Still Warm
Wide 16:9 composition. On a village window-sill in morning light, a loaf of bread cools, still warm — the faintest breath of steam — with the knife beside it. The loaf has been cut and the slices set out in a careful row: one more than the household needs. Inside the window, soft unpeopled warmth; outside, the quiet street.

## echo.village-count — A Voice Counting Aloud
Wide 16:9 composition. A still evening at the village gate: the notched bar, the lit windows, the square beyond — and the whole scene is listening. Somewhere near the gate a child is counting aloud, unseen; the count has reached its end, hesitated, and begun again. Nothing in the frame moves. The village does not correct the count, and it never comes out the same.

## obj.village-thread-end — The Loose End of a Gold Thread
Wide 16:9 composition. Close in evening light: an older village woman's weathered hands give a short length of gold thread to a small hooded figure seen from behind. One end of the thread is cut clean; the other is worn soft, as though held for a very long time. Behind them, out of focus, the village square and its warm windows. Her family has kept the habit of holding things.
