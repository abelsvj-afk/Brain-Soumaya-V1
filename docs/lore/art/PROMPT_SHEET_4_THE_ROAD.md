# Lore art — Prompt Sheet 4 (the Road to the Seam)

*From Claude, 2026-10-31, for Muse. The Road to the Seam (seam S-III) is built as a world: a long road under the Station, lamps at even spacing, a nail in the right wall at every stride, chalk strokes low on the left wall, an empty door-frame, an empty Wardens' post with a worn bench, the road going on into fog. Ten new Book entries live there. Same rules as Sheets 1–3 (read `docs/lore/CONTINUITY_BIBLE.md` first; 4 variants each, `NN-short-title-vK.png`; report in `IMAGE_QA_NOTES.md`; flag, don't rule). Numbering continues from §85.*

**Global style line (append verbatim):** mythic space-fantasy concept art, painterly cinematic realism, soft volumetric light, deep indigo-black space with warm gold and pale cyan accents, restrained palette, heavy atmosphere, large sense of scale, no text, no logos, no UI, no watermarks, no real-world religious symbols.

**Never (this sheet):** NO figure ever shown walking away or lying down — **we never show where Teru ended**, and he is never seen on the road. No readable writing: chalk marks are strokes, never letters or numerals. No grave, no corpse. Calend is **older and grey** (Bible), Mara as a girl only as a small figure from behind or her hand.

### §85 — Where the Road Leaves the Station *(sight.road-mouth)*
16:9. A plain stone arch at the foot of a harbor, the harbor's last lamp behind, and through the arch a long road receding into dark. Cold air, a little warmth. No one in frame. Palette: stone grey, lamp gold, indigo.
### §86 — Lamps at Even Spacing *(sight.road-lamps)*
21:9. A long road under rock walls with lamps on both walls at identical height and spacing, each lit a little ahead of the viewer, the farther ones brighter. Palette: gold, slate, indigo.
### §87 — A Nail at Every Stride *(obj.road-nails)*
3:2. Close on a rock wall with a perfectly straight line of iron nails at shoulder height, each with a faint warm glow, receding into depth. Palette: ember, grey, indigo.
### §88 — Chalk Low on the Wall *(sight.road-chalk)*
3:2. Low on a rock wall, at a child's height, short chalk strokes in small groups running away along the wall — strokes only, no letters. A very small child's hand (only the hand, from behind) mid-stroke. Palette: chalk white, grey, lamp gold.
### §89 — A Door-Frame With Nothing In It *(obj.road-doorframe)*
16:9. A bare door-frame standing against the wall of a road, empty, the hinges bright and freshly oiled, a small oil can on the ground beside it. Palette: warm brown, brass, indigo.
### §90 — The Wardens' Post *(sight.road-post)*
16:9. An iron roadside post and a bench worn pale in two places, swept clean, lamplight on it; no one there. Palette: iron grey, pale wood, gold.
### §91 — Where the Chalk Stops *(sight.road-chalk-end)*
3:2. The same chalk strokes stopping in the middle of a group on the wall — one stroke short — and beyond them clean wall; the road goes on level past it. Palette: chalk white, grey, indigo.
### §92 — Steps Ahead *(echo.road-steps)*
16:9. A long lamp-lit road seen from behind a still viewer (a hooded figure from behind, face never shown, standing still); far ahead, faint footprints of light that keep exactly the same distance. Palette: gold, indigo, pale cyan.
### §93 — A Can of Oil for the Lamps *(obj.road-oil)*
3:2. A small can of lamp oil by the roadside, lid screwed down, sides dry, an older grey-haired traveller's hand (Calend's patchwork cuff) setting it down. Palette: brass, patchwork colours, indigo.
### §94 — The Road Goes On *(sight.road-far)*
21:9. Past the last lamp the road continues level into a fog lit from within; no end visible; calm, not frightening. Palette: pale blue-white, gold, indigo.
