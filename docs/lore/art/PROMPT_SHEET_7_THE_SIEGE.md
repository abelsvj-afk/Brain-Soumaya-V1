# Prompt Sheet 7 — The Siege of the First Light (§§115–124), for Muse
Same style, identity cards and never-list as Sheets 2–6. Four variants each. **No figure appears — not Oru, not a student, not an attacker; no text; nothing says what the Morning was defended from.**

115. **Edges in Ranks** — a broad pale field, crescent edges the height of a person standing rank on rank, each curved like a half-closed smile, all leaning one way; dawn-grey light; nothing broken or stained.
116. **The One That Smiles** — a single taller crescent edge apart from the ranks, curved exactly like a smile, faintly warm-lit.
117. **A Line of Small Cups** — small cups of different sizes on low stones in a line, each full of a thin morning light.
118. **The Bead at the Back** — a bead of light the size of a held breath on a plain plinth; the ranks lean toward it and none touches it.
119. **The Whole Siege on a Table** — a wooden table holding the entire battlefield in miniature, every rank exact.
120. **Ranks Beyond Measure** — faint pale ranks going on into haze, like breath on glass.
121. **A Smear of Pale Light** — a soft edgeless smear of light on the ground, smiling in the way a memory smiles.
122. **A Cord Left Unfinished** — a cord laid straight across the field, staked at both ends, knotted evenly, the last knot untied.
123. **Many Drawing Breath** — an empty field in the stillest hour, the air faintly bowed as if everyone were about to speak.
124. **A Student's Empty Cup** — a small plain cup, empty, held in two gloved hands (hands only).
