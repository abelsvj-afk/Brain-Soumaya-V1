# Prompt Sheet 9 — The Long Morning (§§135–144), for Muse
Same style, identity cards and never-list as Sheets 2–8. Four variants each. **No faces; no figure; no readable text (leaves carry ruled lines only); nothing says whether the Uncounted is Eternity.** Warm pearl-gold light, no horizon line.

135. **A Ring of Leaves, Unbroken** — tall pale ruled leaves in a whole circle on a horizonless gold plain; the lines run on from leaf to leaf.
136. **A Flake of the First Light** — a fingernail-sized flake of bright light on a plain stand.
137. **The People Who Are Light** — tall soft columns of light of every height, unhurried, leaning very slightly toward the viewer; no faces.
138. **A Home of Four Posts** — four plain posts and a roof beam, a lamp lit beneath, a swept threshold, in endless light.
139. **The First Stroke** — a single straight groove crossing the plain out of sight both ways, faintly warm.
140. **The Basin the Record Was Written From** — a perfectly still shallow basin of liquid light.
141. **A Line That Stops** — a curve of light hung at hand height that ends mid-air; more light beyond.
142. **A Leaf Not Yet Written** — one table-sized ruled leaf, blank, lying at the middle of the world.
143. **A Voice Not Quite Spoken** — wide warm light bowed very slightly, as if a language were trying to be small.
144. **A Measure With No Marks** — a plain pale rule held in a traveller's hand (hand only).
