## How to work (same rules as Round 1 — read `docs/lore/CONTINUITY_BIBLE.md` first)
1. **Global style line (append to every prompt, verbatim):** *mythic space-fantasy concept art, painterly cinematic realism, soft volumetric light, deep indigo-black space with warm gold and pale cyan accents, restrained palette, heavy atmosphere, large sense of scale, no text, no logos, no UI, no watermarks, no real-world religious symbols.*
2. **Hard "never" list (from the Bible):** no face for **the Unnamed, the Companion, Pell (the Follower), the player (the Sixteenth), Kith's faceless woman**; **Eternity is MALE, he/him, never a clear face, never a ship**; **Vesh is they/them** with a clean perfectly circular chest-hollow, no gore; the Hush is pale grey **mist**, a soft advance, never an army or fire-smoke; each Firstborn owns **one** ship; the seventh hull has a **blank RECTANGULAR nameplate**; no crescents/crosses as religious symbols; **nothing dies — things rest** (Draft 7 ruling): never a corpse, never gore, never a grave; a "rest" is sleep, fading, a walking-in, a silence.
3. **Faces ARE allowed** for the new canon people below (they have no face restriction): **Hesk, Tamsin, Ossian Reed, Ilsabet Marrow, Wim Ostler, Mara Halvane, Teru Halvane, Maren, Aldous Fenn, Brannoch**. Keep each the same person across all four variants (and across images that reuse them).
4. **Aspect ratios:** the generator ignores ratio, so state it in the prompt text AND tell me which variants came out wrong so I plan crops. Default **16:9 wide** unless marked.
5. **Report per set, like Round 1:** what you checked by eye, what is shaky, what breaks the Bible — in `IMAGE_QA_NOTES.md` — and **do not rule on story questions** (flag them). I (Claude) will pick one canon variant per set by eye and place it in the app's Book. Standing deviations (Eternity's void renders indigo, not white) stay as noted.
6. **Reference faces for returning people:** reuse your own earlier keepers — **Calend** (§8/§15/§29), **Oru** (§14), **Vesh** (§4 v5–v8), **Ysmerel** (§21 v3), **Dorrow Quint** (§24 v4), **Kith** (§26 v4), **Tamsin** (§23 v1), **Hesk** (§42 if generated; else describe from the Bible), **the player** (§11/§27, from behind).

## NEW PROMPTS

### §43 — The Lamp Nobody Pays For *(illustrates the entry "The Gate-post Lamp", Draft 8 road 1)*
Aspect: 16:9
A weathered old woman in a long dark-green scarf stands at an iron gate-post on a night harbor wall, pouring oil into a single small lantern with great care. The lantern is warm and steady; no ship is moored near it and no berth-board lists it. Beyond her, the harbor's lights and a vast dark sky. She does not look up. A coil of rope and a stack of empty oil tins at her feet show she has done this for a very long time. Mood: patient, loving, sad. Palette: dusk blue, warm lantern gold, deep green. Painterly cinematic.

### §44 — Teru Steps Through the First Door *(witness 2, the Harbor Nail; Draft 3 §4)*
Aspect: 16:9
A travel-worn man steps through a tall, narrow, open door standing alone on a bare harbor, a small iron nail held in his closed hand; the doorframe glows faintly warm. A wiry man in a long patchwork coat of many faded colours, with one patch left unfinished, stands beside the door with one palm on the frame and does not touch him. Behind the door, nothing but sky and the first low lamps of what will become a harbor. Mood: arrival, hollowness, a kindness that cannot fix it. Palette: warm amber, pale cyan, dusk.

### §45 — Hesk's Locked Room *(Draft 7; the belongings of the Faded)*
Aspect: 16:9
A big stern man in a Warden's coat, a ring of small keys at his belt, stands in a long narrow store-room lit by one hanging lamp, holding a tin soldier and polishing it with a cloth. On shelves and hooks all around: hundreds of small belongings, each neatly labelled with a blank tag — a single boot, a ribbon, a child's mitten, a coat, a cup. Dust has been wiped from every one. He looks at the toy with an expression of effort, as though he is trying to remember. Mood: guarding, tender, not cruel. Palette: warm brown, amber lamp, cold blue at the doorway.

### §46 — Three Parts of One *(Draft 7: Linnet, Kith, Tamsin, Hesk — a triptych)*
Aspect: triptych, 3 equal vertical panels, 21:9 total
LEFT: a long coat hanging alone on a hook in a store-room, a single lamp on it, a small child's drawing of a faceless woman pinned beside it. CENTER: a half-transparent young woman, watercolour-soft at the edges, asleep on a narrow cot in a quiet ward, a bowl of soup cooling beside her, a green-aproned hand just leaving the frame. RIGHT: a small child in a patched dress, seen from behind, setting one extra place at a long table. Same warm-gold light across all three; nothing connects them but the light. Mood: one person, kept in three places. Palette: warm gold, pale grey, soft green.

### §47 — Tamsin Beside a Laughing Patient *(Draft 7: Ruel)*
Aspect: 16:9
In a quiet ward of narrow cots, a half-transparent man, watercolour-soft at the edges, sits up in bed laughing quietly at something unseen, his laugh visible as a soft ring of warm light. A middle-aged woman in a rolled-sleeve green apron, her back half-turned and her hand to her mouth, stands at the foot of his cot holding a bowl. She is both moved and bracing. Rows of other pale patients sleep behind them under ember lanterns. Mood: love that must remember to let someone return. Palette: pale green, ember gold, soft grey.

### §48 — Aldous's Alcove *(Draft 7: the open alcove; Aldous rests in the Silence)*
Aspect: 16:9
A tall gentle old man in a pale-gold choir robe stands quietly inside an open stone alcove in a vast silent archive, holding a hymnal against his chest with one page cleanly torn out; his mouth is closed, his eyes at peace. He is faintly translucent, as though resting at the edge of being heard. Behind him, empty choir stalls in rows; in front of him, a pale hush gathers softly on the floor, never touching him. Mood: a refusal that cost a voice, and a rest. Palette: pale gold, dusk violet, hushed grey. **He is resting, not dead — no body, no grave.**

### §49 — Ysmerel Hums the Gap *(road 13)*
Aspect: 16:9
A tall, weathered woman in pale-gold choir robes stands before a silent hall of pale, translucent singers, her mouth closed mid-breath, eyes closed; one hand held flat at her chest as if keeping a note she is not singing. A visitor, seen only as a small hooded figure from behind, stands in the aisle with their mouth just open as if to hum. A shaft of gold light falls on the single empty stall. Mood: a held breath. Palette: pale gold, indigo, mist.

### §50 — Dorrow's Blank Line *(Draft 7: the unrecorded debt)*
Aspect: 16:9
A heavy man in a burgundy velvet coat with gold trim sits at an enormous open ledger, his famous trader's smile on his face but not in his eyes; his pen hovers over one single blank line at the very bottom of the page, left empty, while every other line above it is filled with fine, illegible script. Ledger shelves rise into shadow behind him; brass lamps. He is not looking at the blank line — everyone else would. Mood: a debt he will not look at. Palette: burgundy, gold, shadow. **No readable writing anywhere.**

### §51 — Wim and the Empty Cradle *(road 3; the Mooring Payments)*
Aspect: 16:9
A young, tidy junior clerk in a neat Ledger coat stands at the edge of a swept, oiled, empty drydock cradle on a vast harbor, holding a berth-log in one hand and a small unsigned payment slip in the other; the cradle is clean and ready for a ship that is not expected. A single mooring ring on a post beside him glows faintly warm. He looks at the cradle with confusion and tenderness. Mood: faithful, quiet, a little sad. Palette: dusk blue, warm brass, soft gold.

### §52 — Pell Walks in the Late Light *(Draft 7: Pell and Sahl's star)*
Aspect: 16:9
A slight hooded figure in a short jacket, **seen only from behind and in shadow, face never shown**, walks along the edge of a quiet dock toward the glow of a distant single star whose light seems to arrive in one long slow ribbon across the water. In one hand a ribbon, a coin and a folded note, left on a bollard behind her as she goes. A small, dark, unlit skiff moors nearby with no running lights. Mood: always arriving just after. Palette: dusk, pale cyan, a single warm star.

### §53 — Ossian Tells It Wrong *(road 5)*
Aspect: 16:9
A wiry storyteller with a grin that has truth underneath it stands on a crate in a harbor concourse in a patchwork many-pocketed coat, one hand held out as if offering a coin that is not his, telling a story to a small crowd of travellers who lean in. One word seems to hover above his head, slightly the wrong colour among the others, drifting like a loose thread. Behind him, a crooked patchwork ship with a mismatched sail-fin at its berth. Mood: playful, loving, a little sad. Palette: warm patchwork colours against deep blue.

### §54 — Ilsabet's Lane *(road 6)*
Aspect: 16:9
A patient, ink-stained woman unrolls a long survey chart across a drafting table beside a harbor window; the lane drawn on it runs to a thin sealed seam on the Station wall and **stops** — and beyond the seam she is drawing, in pencil, a pale continuation. The chart's ink is subtly shifting as it unrolls. A wide patient survey-barge floats outside. Mood: an edge, drawn gently. Palette: warm paper, ink-blue, pale pencil-grey.

### §55 — Maren's Winter *(Draft 7: Maren rests)*
Aspect: 16:9
A weathered woman in green-gold robes sits at rest on the roots of an enormous tree on a terrace at the end of a long cold season, a stoppered watering can beside her, her head bowed and peaceful; frost on the ground around her, and at the tree's lowest bough a single bare branch pointing quietly toward the sky. Faint new green at the very edge of the frame. She is asleep, not dead — no body, no grave. A small, careful figure in the far distance (the Keeper, from behind) waits at the terrace edge. Mood: a rest and an inheritance. Palette: frost blue, green-gold, pale dawn.

### §56 — Iselle's One Dawn *(witness 4, the Dawn Leaf)*
Aspect: 16:9
A single garden bed held at the exact instant of sunrise: a young gardener kneels in a small plot where every leaf and petal glows with the colours of a dawn that never finishes becoming day, while the sky behind her stays half-dark. She is gently reaching toward an empty patch of soil in the middle of the bed that she has left unplanted. Mood: tender, tragic, unfinished. Palette: dawn rose, pale gold, soft green, indigo edge.

### §57 — The Forty-Second *(Draft 7: Kith's line)*
Aspect: 16:9
A long line of forty-one figures in plain travelling clothes, all joined by a single glowing thread passing from hand to hand, walk in a quiet column into a tall luminous doorway. At the back, one figure — a woman — has stopped on the threshold with her hand still half-extended to the last hand in the line, looking back toward a small village of lit windows behind her. Mood: a choice to stay, the one who didn't. Palette: warm gold thread, pale white doorway, deep blue night.

### §58 — Brannoch and the Mooring Ring *(witness 3)*
Aspect: 16:9
A heavy, calm man sits at the end of a long stone quay on a coil of rope beside a mooring ring set in the stone, one hand resting on it; tied to the ring is a length of rope that runs into the water and ends in nothing. The ring glows faintly warm. A few lights of a distant harbor behind him. His smile is patient; he has been here a very long time and wants nothing. Mood: a stay. Palette: dusk grey-blue, warm gold at the ring.

### §59 — Sahl Hangs a Star *(witness 11)*
Aspect: 16:9
A lone figure on a high rock hangs a single small star on a hook against the night, reaching up with both hands, lit only by the star's own light. Far across the dark below, tiny and distant, a faint figure on a road looks up. The star's light falls in a long slow ribbon that does not yet reach them. Mood: care for a stranger. Palette: indigo, a single warm star, pale cyan thread.

### §60 — Noor Asks Vesh *(witness 13; Draft 3)*
Aspect: 16:9
In a quiet archive alcove, a tall androgynous figure in hooded dusk-violet robes with a clean circular hollow through the chest (no gore) and a young archivist with ink on her fingers sit facing one another on low stools. The archivist has just asked a question; the tall figure's hands are open in their lap, empty. Between them on the stone floor a small round seal and a paper boat. Mood: an unanswered question, kindly. Palette: dusk violet, pale grey, one candle's gold. **Vesh is they/them, androgynous; face gentle, ungendered.**

### §61 — Sethane's Cord *(Draft 7: Sethane's rest)*
Aspect: 16:9
A long knotted cord drifts from a tiny, real-sized station in the sky across a wide field of planets; every knot is tied except the last, which hangs open. At the far end of the cord, small and distant, a tall slim dark-clad woman stands with her back to us at the edge of a vast pale gap, a ring of measuring arms around her, measuring it. Mood: a measure that doesn't close. Palette: steel blue, pale gold cord, deep space. (Sethane faces away; this is the last place she was seen.)

### §62 — The Dig-Warden Counts the Strata *(road 8)*
Aspect: 16:9
A woman with a chalk-stained apron kneels at a vast layered cliff of stone in the Deep Past, pressing her fingertip to each stratum in turn, counting under her breath; where the sixth layer should be, the face of the cliff turns smoothly from one page to the next with no line between them. A small child beside her has drawn seven chalk lines on the rock and is puzzled by one. Mood: a gap in the record. Palette: warm stone, pale dawn, dust.

### §63 — The Sleepwalker's Cup *(Draft 7: the dock lamp)*
Aspect: 16:9
At dawn on a harbor pier, a woman in a pale nightgown stands holding a plain ceramic cup in both hands, gazing at the first light; the dock's lantern behind her is lit, tended. Her face has just become only a woman's face again; she does not know where she is, and is quietly content. A second, empty cup sits on the pier beside the lantern. Mood: waking after a long night. Palette: dawn rose, pale gold, soft blue.

### §64 — Oru's Bead *(Draft 7: Oru and recall)*
Aspect: 16:9
An old, calm, slight man in pale robes holds out to a small hooded witness (seen from behind) a glowing bead of morning light the size of a pearl; his other hand is at his forehead as if trying to remember something. Around the bead the light has the faint shapes of a younger student's outline, half-erased. The bead is warm and gets brighter toward the witness. Mood: handing on what he is losing. Palette: pale gold, dawn white, soft blue.

## BATCH 2 — the Heavens (Draft 9) — §§65–72
*Same rules as above. These paint the sky bodies that carry lore (see `docs/lore/DRAFT_9_THE_HEAVENS.md`). Wide 16:9, four variants each; no faces needed (a distant figure may appear only where stated).*

### §65 — Voyager and the Late Signal
A tiny dark probe far out in deep space, its long thin antenna folded, trailing behind it — in the direction it *came from*, not where it is going — a single long pale ribbon of light. Far behind that, small and faint, a hooded figure (seen from behind, face never shown) walks along a quiet shore of stars, following the ribbon. Mood: always almost there. Palette: indigo-black, pale cyan ribbon, a single warm point at the horizon.

### §66 — The Pale World and the Gap
A cool pale-blue world hung in the dark beyond a harbor's lights. On its night side a ring of dim city-lights runs all the way round — except one small clean dark gap in the ring, the exact shape of a single locked room. A Warden's patrol-ship passes in the foreground, turning its head away. Mood: where someone came from, and doesn't say. Palette: pale blue, ash grey, one gold patrol light.

### §67 — The Furnace and the Nail
A red-black lava world with glowing seams, seen from a harbor wall at night; in the foreground a wiry man in a long patchwork coat holds a small iron nail up to the planet's light, its tip glowing the same amber as the world's seams. His other hand rests on a standing door-frame. Mood: where a warmth came from, maybe. Palette: furnace red, amber, dusk blue.

### §68 — Far Home and the Tables
A blue world and its pale moon hung low on a dark horizon over a wide field of many small cottage tables; every table is laid for one more than sat, and every extra place faces the blue world. Warm lantern-light on each table. Mood: missing, not mourning. Palette: warm gold, soft blue, night green.

### §69 — The Slow Garden (the Pillars)
Towering columns of cold blue-gold dust in space, their tips each glowing with a held, unfinished dawn; a small ship sits at a respectful distance, no one visible. Mood: patience, unfinished becoming. Palette: dawn rose, deep blue, gold.

### §70 — The Honest Hole
A black hole drawn with a perfect, clear ring of light at its edge, and in front of it, small and seen from behind, a sharp-tongued-looking figure in a plain coat standing with arms folded as if comparing it to something not in the frame. Mood: an edge shown plainly. Palette: orange ring, indigo, cold white.

### §71 — The Fifteen and the Dark Sixteenth (over the witness tree)
A huge old tree on a terraced hill at night, one bare bough pointing; above it a starfield in which fifteen warm stars are faintly brighter and form a loose arc, and in the arc's end, exactly one place where a star should be is dark. Mood: counting, one short. Palette: night blue, gold stars, deep green.

### §72 — Saturn's Extra Gap
Saturn at true size, its many rings turning at different paces like joined hands, and in one ring a single thin dark division that no moon explains, just wide enough for one more hand. Mood: someone turned back. Palette: pale gold, steel blue.
