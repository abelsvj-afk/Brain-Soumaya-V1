# Continuity Bible — who is who (for every image, voice and line of text)

*Source of truth for IDENTITY: gender/pronouns, age, build, signature things, ships. Written 2026-10-30 from the locked lore (Drafts 1–6). If an image, voice or line disagrees with this sheet, the sheet wins; if the sheet disagrees with the lore, the lore wins and this sheet gets fixed. Anything once marked **PROPOSED** was silent in the lore and began as Claude's choice; **the six identity defaults (Maren, Nuaré, Tamsin, Ossian Reed, Pell the Follower, Wim Ostler) were CONFIRMED by the owner on 2026-10-30** and are no longer proposals — treat them as settled canon.*

## 0. The five things that must never be wrong
1. **Eternity is MALE — he / him / his.** The owner's own words: "he is and always was … he created." Vast, pale, radiant, face serene and impossible to describe, but unmistakably a man's presence: broad-shouldered, tall, calm. **Never female, never "they", never androgynous.** He has **no ship** ("he is where ships go"). He is a Maker, not a god-of-any-real-religion: no halo copied from a religion, no cross/crescent/etc.
2. **The Unnamed is a WOMAN — she / her.** One of the first of the made. She has **no ship**, is **not a Firstborn**, and her face is **never shown or described** (Draft 3 "never say her name"). She **laughed too loud** (the one surviving trait). Her only physical form in the world is the smooth, plain, opal-white relic **the Witness Without a Name**.
3. **Vesh is they / them** — androgynous, gentle, tired, kind, hooded dusk-violet robes; a clean, soft, perfectly circular hollow **through the chest** (no blood, no gore). Flies **the Lullaby**. (Draft 1 uses "they" for Vesh throughout.)
4. **Each Firstborn flies exactly ONE ship and each ship has ONE owner** (table §2). Do not swap, merge or invent.
5. **Soumaya is the player's companion/ship, not a Firstborn.** Her ship is the *seventh hull*: it echoes the six Firstborn ships and has a **blank rectangular nameplate** on the flank. Never give her a name painted there.

## 3. Relationships Muse must keep straight
- Eternity **created**; nothing is above him. The Firstborn were made **to see, not to serve**.
- **Vesh erased the Unnamed** (she asked, out of being kept) — Vesh did it out of mercy and is **sympathetic**, not a villain. Vesh and the Unnamed were close (Vesh's whole aspect was Mercy).
- **Calend = the Wayfarer** (walks among the made). **The Surveyor is Sethane's apprentice.** **The Keeper is Maren's heir.** **The Archivist is Vesh's penitent.** **Nuaré founded the Open Hand.**
- **Kith is a child of a Faded; Hesk keeps the Faded's belongings; Tamsin keeps the Faded** (they have never spoken).
- **Ysmerel is secretly a Sitter** (never shown plainly); **Dorrow pays the debt through Wim**.
- **The player is the SIXTEENTH witness** (a small hooded figure seen from behind — no face, no gender shown).

## 4. Rules for every image
- No faces for: the Unnamed, the Companion, the Follower, the player, Kith's faceless woman (drawn without a face).
- Eternity: **male**, white-on-white. Vesh: **they**, androgynous, chest hollow. The Hush: pale grey **mist**, a soft advance — **not** an army, not smoke from fire.
- No real-world religious iconography (crosses, crescents, haloes copied from any religion).
- Style: mythic space-fantasy concept art, painterly cinematic realism, indigo-black space with warm gold + pale cyan accents.

## 5. When in doubt
Flag it, don't guess: list the conflict in your report and keep the image out of the "keepers" until the owner answers. Kinds of conflicts to flag: a pronoun the sheet doesn't cover, two ships that look alike, a relationship the sheet doesn't state, a prompt whose wording admits two readings.
