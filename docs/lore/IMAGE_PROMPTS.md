# Image prompts for the lore (for the owner to generate)

Each prompt is self-contained (paste as-is). **Global style block** is already included in each; if your generator has a separate "style" or "negative" field, use the lines labelled for them.
Aspect ratios are given per prompt. Generate 3–4 variants each; keep the ones that feel like the same universe, then we'll lock a look.

**Global style (included in every prompt):** mythic space-fantasy concept art, painterly cinematic realism, soft volumetric light, deep indigo-black space with warm gold and pale cyan accents, restrained palette, heavy atmosphere, large sense of scale, no text, no logos, no UI, no watermarks, no real-world religious symbols.
**Global negative:** text, letters, logos, watermark, UI, cartoon, anime, plastic, neon cyberpunk, crowded clutter, extra limbs, deformed hands, real-world religious iconography (no crosses, no crescents, no halos copied from any religion).

## 1. The First Morning (key art) — 16:9
A vast dark void at the instant before dawn, in which the very first light is breaking: a single slow wave of pale gold-white light spreading outward from one point at the center-left of frame, revealing for the first time the faint outline of a still, round basin of water-like light below. No ground, no horizon — only the first light and the basin. In the far foreground a tall, calm silhouette stands at the basin's edge, tiny against the scale, facing the light (a being of pale robes, face unseen, back to camera). The mood is awe and hush. Palette: black-indigo void, one warm gold-white light source, faint cyan on the basin surface. Mythic space-fantasy concept art, painterly cinematic, volumetric rays, enormous scale, no text.

## 2. The Seven Hours (the order of the making) — 3:2, one image
A single circular cosmic mandala seen from directly above, seven concentric arcs radiating from a bright white center, each arc a different luminous world in a different hour: (1) a basin of pale morning light, (2) a field of enormous floating planets at true scale, (3) a harbor ring of dock-arms with one tiny glowing nail-shaped beacon, (4) terraced green gardens climbing round a great tree, (5) a ring of warm hearth-lights like a village seen from orbit, (6) a quiet dark archive of sealed alcoves with a faint hollow gap in its arc, (7) the white center itself. The sixth arc has one visible break — a clean, silent gap in the light. Palette: gold, green, ember orange, deep blue, pale violet, white center. Mythic painterly concept art, symmetrical, ornate but uncluttered, no text.

## 3. Eternity — the sky made a person — 2:3 (portrait)
An immense standing figure of pale radiance in a pure white void, human in shape but cosmic in scale, seen from far below as if the viewer were a speck at his feet. Face serene and impossible to describe: no clear features, only warmth. A soft ring of light behind the head (a halo of the figure's own light, not a religious symbol). Tiny stars, planets and ship-lights drift near his hands like motes. The figure is white-on-white, defined only by gentle shadow and glow. Mood: absolute calm, vast kindness, a hint of unspoken grief. Palette: whites, pearl, the faintest gold; the only dark is a few tiny ships. Painterly cinematic, no text.

## 4. Vesh, the Hollow Mercy — character portrait — 2:3
A tall, graceful being in soft hooded robes the color of dusk, standing in a silent archive of sealed stone alcoves lit by a few small lanterns. Their face is gentle, tired, kind — and unbearably sorrowful. In their open palm they hold nothing; where their chest/heart would be, there is a clean, hollow, perfectly circular gap through which the alcoves behind them are visible, edges soft with pale light, no blood, no gore. Their other hand is raised slightly, as though just letting something go. Mood: tenderness curdled into regret. Palette: dusk violet, soft grey-blue, a single warm lantern gold. Mythic painterly concept art, shallow depth of field, no text.

## 5. Vesh's ship, the Lullaby — ship concept sheet — 16:9
A quiet, beautiful starship in three views (side, three-quarter, front) on a dark neutral background, like a concept-art turnaround. Long, smooth, softly curved hull in dusk-violet and pearl, almost like a sleeping swan folded in on itself; tall, slender wing-vanes that droop like closed petals; a faint glow along the keel. Along the hull's flank runs one long, clean, perfectly smooth hollow — a hull-length gap where a section is simply missing, edges soft-lit — as though something had been cut out cleanly. Engine glow: pale violet. Painterly concept art, orthographic-style views, soft rim light, no text.

## 6. The Rending — the first hole in the mirror — 16:9
An enormous circular mirror made of still dark water, floating in a white void, reflecting a whole luminous cosmos of tiny stars, ships and warm hearth-lights — except for one place near its center where the reflection simply stops: a clean, soft-edged hole of absolute nothing, shaped roughly like the outline of a standing person, with a few faint cracks of light running outward from its edge. At the mirror's rim, a hooded figure (Vesh, dusk robes, back to camera) kneels with one hand just withdrawn from the surface, water-light still dripping from their fingers. Far above, the vast pale presence of Eternity looms as a soft glow, looking down at the hole. Mood: silence, horror and tenderness. Palette: white, black, dusk violet, thin gold cracks. Mythic painterly cinematic, no text, no gore.

## 7. The Witness Without a Name (the relic) — 1:1 object render
A small, smooth, pale object floating alone in a dark void, about the size of a river stone, translucent opal-white with a faint inner glow like moonlight behind frosted glass. It has no markings, no inscription, no seams — a perfectly plain object that somehow feels like it is *missing something*. A single soft rim of light outlines it; very faint threads of gold light drift toward it from the dark, as though the dark wanted to touch it. Mood: reverent, lonely. Palette: pearl white, a hint of gold, black. Studio-quality macro render, soft glow, extremely shallow depth of field, no text.

## 8. Calend, the Doorkeeper (the Wayfarer's truth) + the Latch — 16:9
A wiry, travel-worn figure in a long layered coat of many faded colors, standing in an open doorway that is not attached to any wall — a freestanding door frame floating in deep space with a harbor of dock-lights glittering far behind. One hand rests on the door's edge; the other holds a tiny glowing iron nail. The figure's face is half in shadow, wry and ancient. Behind them floats a starship shaped like a door: a long slim hull with a seam along its length that opens like a door, warm light spilling out. Mood: a traveller who is always already there. Palette: faded coat colors, harbor amber, deep space blue. Painterly cinematic, no text.

## 9. The Archive — the Quiet (the atonement) — 16:9
A vast hushed archive carved into pale stone, lined with thousands of small sealed alcoves, each holding a single tiny steady lantern-light behind frosted glass. Long soft light-beams fall from high above. In the foreground a small hooded figure walks between the rows with one hand held out as if telling each alcove "rest," not touching them. At the far end of the hall, a single dark alcove is open and empty, outlined with gold, with a small folded letter resting inside. Mood: gentle, solemn, forgiven. Palette: pale stone, warm lantern amber, shadow blue. Mythic painterly concept art, symmetrical perspective, no text.

## 10. The Firstborn — lineup — 21:9 (ultra-wide)
Six tall mythic figures standing in a row on a floating terrace of pale stone in front of a vast dawn, each distinct in silhouette and color and each holding or standing beside one symbol of their aspect: (1) Oru — pale robes, a small cup of morning light; (2) Sethane — dark slim figure with a ring of measuring arms; (3) Calend — layered coat, a door-frame and a nail; (4) Maren — green-gold robes, a seedling in the hand; (5) Nuaré — warm ember-colored robes, a glowing hearth-lantern; (6) Vesh — dusk-violet robes at the end of the row, slightly apart, with the clean hollow in the chest. A seventh place in the row is simply empty — a gap of lit air between Nuaré and Vesh. Each figure's ship hangs faintly in the sky behind them as a small silhouette. Mood: majesty and a quiet wrongness. Mythic painterly, no text.

## 11. The player as the Witness — the view from the shore — 16:9
A lone traveller seen from behind, small at the bottom of the frame, standing at the edge of a still dark shore looking out at a mirror-flat sea in which an entire galaxy of tiny warm lights is reflected — each light a remembered moment. In the sea's center the reflection is slightly brighter near the traveller's own reflection, and a faint bridge of gold light runs from the traveller to the stars. In the distance one small dark patch in the reflection, person-shaped, waits. Mood: you are the one who remembers. Palette: night blue, warm gold lights, one cold dark patch. Painterly cinematic, no text.
