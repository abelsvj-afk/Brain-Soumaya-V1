# DRAFT 9 — The Heavens: every body in the sky has a story

*PROPOSED by Claude, 2026-10-31, from the owner's request: "literally every celestial body, every planet outside of your user's individual galaxy … how every planet is connected to the story, what the lore is around those planets, and how they are involved in lore … it wasn't just the lore itself, it was also the environment in most of these video games that gets you immersed."*
**Status: PROPOSED — strictly additive.** Nothing here changes a locked word of Drafts 1–8 or the Bible. Every connection is a **new reading of something already in the sky**, tagged **[NEW]**; each can be struck alone.

**What "outside your own galaxy" means here.** The player's own brain is the home galaxy (private, unreachable by anyone else). Everything in the **worlds** — the Station's sky, the Garden, the Deep Past, Scale, the Hearths, the Archive, Eternity — is *the shared heavens*: the remembered life of every witness, kept by Eternity and shown back (Draft 1 §0). So a body in those skies is not scenery; it is **something someone remembered**. That is the rule that makes all of this one story.

---

## 1. The Sky Rules (why every body can carry lore without changing the lore)
1. **A body is a remembered thing.** Each planet, probe and nebula is *the thing a witness refused to forget*, kept at true size. (Draft 4: the worlds are the player's remembered life; the sky is everyone else's.)
2. **Two accounts, never one.** Surveyors call a body one thing; the people it touched call it another. The Book never says which is right.
3. **Every body faces one person.** A body is tied to a character from Draft 7/8 by *an echo* (same shape, same silence, same number). The player notices; nobody says.
4. **Environment tells it first.** Each body has an **in-scene clue** (a ring, a scar, a ribbon on a probe, a ledger-line that doesn't balance) readable *before* any text. The text only confirms what the eye already half-knew (item description after environment, as in the games the owner means).
5. **Nothing dies; things rest.** A star that ended *rested*. A probe that stopped *rests*. A world that went dark *is resting*.
6. **No number is ever the point.** Sizes appear as proportion ("larger than the grief"), never as a statistic the player must chase.

## 2. The Gazetteer — by world

### 2.4 THE DEEP PAST — the First Hour's sky (Hour I, Oru's world)
**First Light (the basin)** — *the place where the first morning was kept.* Tied to: **Oru**, **Orsolya**. Clue: its floor holds a pale warmth a hand can feel and an eye cannot. Rumour: *"He greets you by name. Do not ask how he knows it."*
**The Anchor Spire** — *a spire driven into the oldest layer to hold the rest in place.* **[NEW]** Tied to: **Maren/the Keeper** (leaning on what holds). Clue: its glowing tip faces the missing stratum. Rumour: *"Everyone leans on it. It has never leaned back."*
**The Spinning Heart (the orb)** — *a sphere of first light turning slowly.* **[NEW]** Tied to: **Oru's bead**. Clue: it turns once for every recall you make elsewhere (presentation). Rumour: *"The warmest thing in the descent, and it gives no explanation."*
**The Remnant (supernova)** — *a star that ended long ago.* Tied to: **the Siege** and **Aldous's silence**. Clue: rings of colour, pale. Rumour: *"The loudest quiet in the Deep Past."*

### 2.5 THE GROWING PLACE — the Garden's sky (Hour IV, Maren's world)
No planets: its sky is **a plain starfield and the witness tree**. **[NEW]** The stars above the tree are not random: **fifteen of them are faintly brighter** (the witnesses) and **one place where a star should be is dark** (the sixteenth). Clue: draw a line between any two and the dark one is always on it. Rumour: *"The tree counts them every night. It always comes to one fewer than it hopes."*

### 2.6 QUIET / ARCHIVE — Vesh's sky (Hour VI)
Its sky is **always dusk, never night**, with **very few stars**. **[NEW]** The stars that are there are *pinpricks of paper-boat white* — one for each page Vesh could not leave in the dark. Clue: a few of them drift down toward the low pool and rest on it as small pale boats. Rumour: *"Not one has sunk."*

### 2.7 ETERNITY — the Standing (Hour VII)
There are no bodies. There is **the white**, **the far shell**, **the dye clouds**, **fifteen lamps** and **the lit sixteenth that is not lit**. **[NEW]** The far veils are the **colours of the other six Hours' skies**, one per Hour: morning rose, heavens steel, threshold amber, growing green, hearth ember, rest violet — so the worlds' skies are *folded into his*. Clue: when a visitor looks long enough in one direction, that Hour's colour brightens. Rumour: *"He keeps all the skies. Look at which he keeps nearest."*

## 3. The Constellations (named shapes in every shared sky — [NEW])
Each is a **pattern the stars make that the Surveyors drew first and the people kept**; each is a *finding aid* disguised as a name. Seen in every world's sky.
- **The Fifteen** — fifteen warm points in an arc; the sixteenth place dark. *(Draft 3 §4.)*
- **The Door, Half Open** — an upright slit of dark in a bright field, one hand's-breadth wide. *(Calend's door.)*
- **The Hung Star** — one point trailing a faint line toward an empty place. *(Sahl; Pell.)*
- **The Torn Page** — a rectangle of stars with one corner missing in a clean tear. *(Ysmerel's hymnal; Aldous.)*
- **The Seven Hours** — seven rings, the sixth drawn twice. *(Draft 1 §2.)*
- **The Late Boat** — a small dark skiff with no lights. *(Pell's Afterglow.)*
- **The Extra Chair** — a chair-shape with one empty seat. *(Nuaré; Kith.)*
- **The Cord** — a line of dots with one untied end. *(Sethane.)*
Each is a place passage ("Look closely" at the sky, from a parked ship, shows the name and one line) and a Book *sight* later.

## 5. Environment storytelling beyond bodies (the part the owner stressed)
Each world also holds **props and arrangements that carry the story without a word** — the Elden-Ring habit of a *ruin that tells you what happened*:
- **Station:** a berth-board with one blank berth, swept; a gate-post lamp (Mara) lit by no one; a locked annex door with a worn path to it; a tin-whistle bench; ledger stamps on crates that go in a pattern that spells nothing.
- **Deep Past:** chalk tallies of seven on a cliff, one stroke smudged; a ribbon on a stair post newer than the stair; a child's handprint at the Rim, too high up.
- **Quiet / Archive:** a row of folded paper boats on a ledge, all pointing the same way; a hymnal on a stall with one page torn clean; a cot made up with no one in it.
- **People:** a table laid for one more; a button jar with every size but one; a loaf left at an empty chair; a wall of small drawings, one face-less.
- **Garden:** one terrace never watered; a seed on a plot with a small stone on it; a bough that points.
- **Scale:** a cord with an untied knot; a tape measure left extended; a notebook on a probe's hull.
- **Eternity:** fifteen lamps and a sixteenth with a wick but no flame.
*(All presentational scene dressing — each prop is one cheap mesh or sprite placed from a seed; none changes a rule. Built a few per world per slice.)*
