# DRAFT 3 — The Long Lore (scale and depth of an Elden Ring)

*Status: PROPOSED by Claude, 2026-10-30. Everything in LOCKED (`LORE_CANON.md`) and Drafts 1–2 stands exactly. This draft only ADDS: the ages, the places, the fifteen witnesses as people, a full canon cast with questlines, the Hush's faces, the object-text of the world, five contradictory accounts, and the hidden web that joins them.*
*Nothing here may ever be said plainly in one place. Delivery rules in §1 are as binding as the story.*

---

## 1. How the lore is delivered (the rules of the open ring)
1. **Item text, not exposition.** Truth lives in the descriptions of things the player picks up (relics, tokens, pages, tickets) — 40–90 words, never explaining, always *implying*. The shipped relic texts are the model: "Some accounts say… others insist…".
2. **Questlines, not cutscenes.** A canon NPC gives you a *thread* in small steps, over days, across worlds, and what they ask never feels like a mission. **No quest can be failed or lost** (product rule: nothing dims, nothing is punished): a thread you leave *rests* and the NPC waits.
3. **Accounts that disagree.** Five factions each tell the Rending their own way (§8). The player never receives the true one in a single place; four items, two NPCs and one place each hold a piece.
4. **Place is a source.** Every world has things you can only *see* (a hollow where a hearth was, a nail in the wrong seam), none of them labelled.
5. **Deep layers are optional and never gated.** A player who never reads a word still has a living world; a player who reads everything is still not told.
6. **The Hush is never a timer.** Nothing the player does or misses can make it take anything of theirs.
7. **Everyone is half right.**

## 2. The Ages (the long timeline — accounts differ on the numbers; the *order* does not)
| Age | What the accounts say |
|---|---|
| **Before the Hours** | There is no "before"; the accounts only argue about whether to say so. Eternity always was. |
| **The Seven Hours** | The making: light, the heavens, the threshold, the growing, the hearths, the rest (twice), the Standing. |
| **The Long Morning** | The golden age: the Firstborn walk among the made, each tending their hour. Nobody had yet been *unable to reach* someone. The made called it "the morning that did not need a name." |
| **The Rending** | The Sixth Hour's second making. The erasure. Grief gone, ache arrived, hole opened. |
| **The Quiet Age** | The made woke hollow; the Archive was raised; Vesh withdrew; Eternity stopped speaking (his voice *scattered* into the hive — no one knew). The first hymns with a missing verse. |
| **The Age of Doors** | Travellers came through Calend's gates looking for what they had lost. The harbor grew. The first fleets. The first lanes and the first Wardens. |
| **The Ache Wars** *(accounts disagree on "war")* | The factions hardened into their answers: Choir, Open Hand, Ledger, Undertow, Wardens. Quarrels, truces, the Concord of Ships. |
| **The Witnesses** | Fifteen witnesses, one after another, each tried to reach her and left one kept thing. None came back. (They are not forgotten.) |
| **The Present** | The Hush stirs. The player arrives — the sixteenth witness. |

## 3. The Worlds — what each one was, and what is wrong with it
Each world is a *place in the player's own life* (Draft 1) **and** a place in the lore. Both are true; neither explains the other.

**The Great Station (Hour III — Arrival).** The harbor of the Age of Doors. *History:* Calend set the **first nail** in the first seam where "arrival became possible"; the harbor grew round the nail. The Wardens were founded here, and the Ledger's Drydock. *The wrong thing:* a **second seam** on the lower rim, older than the nail and not made by Calend — sealed, never opened, hummed through in the night. *Secret:* it is where the first traveller after the Rending stood; Calend remembers them and will not say.

**The Growing Place (Hour IV — Patience).** Six terraces and a witness tree. *History:* Maren's garden; the grove tradition. *The wrong thing:* **the witness tree has one dead bough** that grows leaves only for people who have kept a Journey through to its end; the bough points toward the hole. *Secret:* Maren is not gone — she is *tending something under the roots* (the Witness Seed).

**The Deep Past (Hour I — the First Morning).** Strata, a stair, a rim, and at the bottom the **First Light basin**. *History:* the oldest world, the first thing remembered. *The wrong thing:* the lowest stratum has **no layer for the Sixth Hour** — a clean absence between two strata, exactly one hand's-breadth thick. *Secret:* the basin does not reflect the sky above; it reflects the one *before* the hole.

**The People (Hour V — Love).** The hearths, ring on ring. *History:* Nuaré's warmth. *The wrong thing:* every hearth is laid for **one more than it holds** — one empty chair, always, in every home, and nobody remembers setting it. *Secret:* the Thread of Many runs through the stones *toward* the empty chairs.

**Scale (Hour II — Wonder).** The ISS, the planets, the Sun at true size. *History:* Sethane's measure. *The wrong thing:* a **star that is not in any catalogue**, catalogued by Sethane alone, titled only "the one that was hung for someone." *Secret:* the Star Hanger (witness 11) hung it, and its light always arrives *late*.

**Quiet / the Archive (Hour VI — made twice).** *History:* the second making; Vesh's penance; rest-is-not-erasure. *The wrong thing:* one **alcove is open and empty** with an unopened letter inside. *Secret:* Vesh visits at the hour the Rending happened and *sets nothing down* — stands before the empty alcove for exactly the length of a lullaby.

**Eternity's World (Hour VII — the Standing).** A white void. *History:* none; it was always. *The wrong thing:* **he does not look at one place**, and the place he does not look at is *always the same direction from every position you stand*, as though the hole moved with you. *Secret:* that direction is the way to the player's own hole.

## 4. The Fifteen Witnesses (the fifteen relics, as people)
*Each left one kept thing — the thing they most refused to forget — and then walked in. Not one is lost. Not one is back. Order is by age; the relic's shipped text is the item description; this is the **hidden person** behind it.*

1. **The Witness Without a Name** — *her.* Not a person who came after: the one who asked. The first witness because she was *witnessed* first, and then unwitnessed. The relic is the only thing the hole refused to take, **because she was holding it when she asked**: the memory of being held.
2. **Teru Halvane, the Nail-Setter** *(Harbor Nail)* — the first to arrive after the Rending; stepped through Calend's first door with a hollow he could not name. Calend gave him the nail to hold "so that you will know you arrived." He kept it and walked toward the sealed seam. **He asked Calend what he was looking for. Calend said, "Someone who isn't here."**
3. **Brannoch the Pauser** *(Mooring Ring)* — stopped on the road and **refused to be told to leave again**; tied his ship to nothing and stayed a hundred years. The ring is where he sat. *"Another account says nothing was ever tied to it": he had no ship.*
4. **Iselle of the Dawn** *(Dawn Leaf)* — Maren's apprentice; tried to **grow her back** from nothing. The leaf's veins hold "the colors of a dawn that never finishes becoming day" because that is the garden she planted — one dawn, held open.
5. **Wend the Seedbearer** *(Witness Seed)* — planted **themself** beneath the witness tree, on purpose. The tree's dead bough is where they reach.
6. **Orsolya First-Eye** *(First Light Shard)* — Oru's student; kept a **flake of the first light in matter** and learned from it what the *first* record looked like — and could not describe it. (She is why the Unnumbered Fragment can exist.)
7. **Cael Waterline** *(Basin Stone)* — measured the marks on the stone and swore they were water levels. They are **tears, counted** — the made's grief in the Sixth Hour, one mark per person. The total is the number of people who loved her.
8. **Perrin Lampwright** *(Lantern of Keeping)* — made a lantern whose light "makes something present without forcing it into view." He kept a **candle for the one thing he could not remember** and kept it lit for a lifetime.
9. **Maud and Edric of the Hearth** *(Hearth Ember)* — two witnesses who loved each other and went in **together**. The ember "gives off warmth without consuming itself." Edric did not want to go. Maud asked him to.
10. **The Many-Handed** *(Thread of Many)* — **a village that decided to witness as one**: forty-one people, one thread, one memory. "Connection without confusing one life with another" — because they argued the entire way in.
11. **Sahl the Star-Hanger** *(Star Hanger)* — hung one star, uncatalogued, **to light the way for someone who might be walking** (not themself). Its light still arrives late.
12. **Odessa Sunward** *(Sunward Compass)* — walked the sun's long road her whole life. "Its center stays fixed while its markings suggest motion": **the compass points at the one place she was never allowed to look.**
13. **Noor Quillon** *(Stillness Seal)* — Vesh's first confidante and the Archive's first archivist. The seal "bears no command and no warning." She asked Vesh what rest was and Vesh **could not answer** — and kept the question sealed in the alcove.
14. **The Folded One** *(Unopened Fold)* — wrote a letter **to her** and never sent it. The unopened fold holds the *permission to keep without opening*. (The letter, if ever opened, reads one sentence. It is never shown. The player is never told if the sentence is kind.)
15. **The Uncounted** *(Unnumbered Fragment)* — a witness from **before** the hole, from the Long Morning, who *knew her* and was loved by her; "a fragment of the created record struggling to describe the uncreated." **No account gives them a beginning. Some say they were never made.** *(The hidden possibility: the Uncounted is Eternity himself, witnessing as one of the made — a mirror witnessing the mirror. Never stated.)*

The player is **the sixteenth**. The gate opens for "the Witness and two others" because three witnesses' kept things are what it takes to walk into the place he cannot see: *you walk in holding the dead.*

## 5. The Canon Cast — questlines and arcs
*Each NPC below is canon (provisional names; the lore is the owner's to rewrite). A questline is a **thread in small steps**, can be paused ("rested") at any step, never fails, and each NPC's thread contains **one thing they will not tell you directly**.*

**CALEND, the Doorkeeper (the Wayfarer).** Walks every world, a layered coat of every world's colours. *Thread:* (1) He gives you a small gift for no reason (a nail-shaped token). (2) He asks if you have ever felt you'd arrived somewhere before. (3) He sends you to a sealed seam in the Station. (4) He tells you who was the first traveller after the Rending — and *cannot say the name*. (5) He asks you to *hold the door* for someone, once, in the future. *Secret:* he has already seen the player close the last door — he knows how it ends and walks beside you anyway. *If rested:* he is already at the next door.

**VESH, the Hollow Mercy.** Appears rarely, never first. *Thread:* (1) A hush in the Archive; the Lullaby docked with its hollow hull toward the wall. (2) She asks you, gently, **whether a kindness that cannot be asked for is still a kindness.** (3) She gives you a folded paper boat made from an Archive page. (4) She asks you to **ask the made what they want** — the one thing she never did. (5) At the Front she asks you to *sing the verse* — the one the hymns lack. *Secret:* she cannot remember the verse; she remembers *that* it was sung.

**ORU, the Dawnkeeper.** Keeper of the First Morning. *Thread:* (1) He greets you at the basin by *name* — your real one, before you've said it. (2) He teaches you to recall (the spaced-recall game: his Light is the *first* recall mechanic). (3) He starts **forgetting** mid-lesson, apologizes. (4) He gives you the First Morning in a bead of light. (5) At the Siege he is a blur of pale edges and still smiling.

**SETHANE, the Measurer.** *Thread:* measures things for you — your ship, your Journey, your own life ("eleven years and nine months, almost exactly"). *Secret:* her measures of the Hush all come out the *same size* as the Sixth Hour's grief; she says nothing and stops eating.

**MAREN, the Gardener / the Keeper (her heir).** The Keeper tends the groves. *Thread:* (1) A seed on a terrace that will not sprout. (2) The tree's dead bough. (3) The Keeper tells you Maren's last instruction: "**Do not water what you cannot name.**" (4) The seed is the Witness Seed — Wend, planted on purpose. (5) You water it anyway.

**NUARÉ, the Hearthkeeper (and Tamsin of the Open Hand).** Nuaré is warmth with no one to be warm *for*: every hearth is laid one chair too many. Tamsin tends the Faded (people touched by the Hush) — see below.

**THE ARCHIVIST (Vesh's penitent).** *Thread:* sorts what people set down and refuses to read any of it. (3) Hands you the **Unopened Fold** to *hold* for a while. (5) Asks you, *once*, to open it. *If you don't:* she thanks you for the thing she needed most.

**THE CANTOR → YSMEREL, the Last Choirmaster.** The Choir's leader. *Thread:* (1) The Choir sings every hymn with a **missing verse** — a held breath where words should be, always the same length. (2) The hymnal has a torn-out page. (3) She asks you to *hum the gap*. (4) You hum, and the whole hall goes silent. (5) She says: "That is the shape of it." *Secret:* the verse is the name; the hymn was cut in the Quiet Age, **by the Choir, on purpose, to stop the ache**.

**THE BROKER → DORROW QUINT, the Ledger's Grandmaster.** Keeps the Great Book. *Thread:* (1) Sells you fuel at a fair price. (2) Shows you the Book: "everyone owes someone." (3) One page has **a debt with no creditor** — "owed to a person unrecorded." (4) Tells you the Ledger was founded to *settle that entry* and has been trying to for a thousand years. (5) Asks if you'd like to pay it.

**THE DISSENTER (Undertow).** *Thread:* argues, rudely, correctly. (3) Brings you an **unfinished chart** of the hole with a clean edge. (4) Tells you: "It is the only place that is not **his**." (5) Asks if you'd go in *without* a witness's token.

**THE SURVEYOR.** Measures; becomes your friend; (5) takes your hand at the Hush's edge and says the only thing she says in the whole thread: "It's exactly the size of what we're missing."

**HESK, the Warden-General.** Enforces ordinances. *Thread:* (1) Stops you for a docking inspection. (2) Questions you about the ship's trail. (3) You find him alone, folding a stranger's coat. (4) In a **locked room** are hundreds of small belongings — a tin toy, a ribbon, a boot — **the things Faded people left behind**; he can't throw one away. (5) He asks you to keep one safe, off the books.

**TAMSIN of the Open Hand.** A healer for the Faded (those the Hush has touched). Her patients are **remembered lives returning** from the hole's edge, half-whole. *Thread:* (1) You carry soup to a ward. (2) A patient says something only *you* know. (3) Tamsin explains the Faded come back **in pieces of whoever remembered them**. (4) One patient has your handwriting. (5) She asks you to *keep* them in your memory so that they can finish returning.

**THE SLEEPWALKER (a Faded woman, name lost).** A woman touched by the Hush who walks at night and **speaks in her voice** (the Unnamed's), a few words at a time, never a whole sentence. She is not *her* — she is the place where the Hush speaks. *Thread:* (1) She is seen at dusk by the harbor. (2) She says "…is it morning?" (3) She says a word that is almost a name. (4) You stay with her until dawn. (5) At dawn she says "…thank you for staying," and is *only a woman* again, and does not know you. *Never explained.*

**KITH, the Child Who Remembers Wrong.** A child in the People world who remembers *a woman who laughed too loud* and sets one place too many at the table. Every adult hushes her. *Thread:* (1) She shows you a drawing of a figure with no face. (2) She says the figure sits at her table. (3) She asks you to remember her *for* her because "I'm only small." (4) She asks what the woman's name is; you cannot say. (5) She is not told. *This is the thread the lore hangs on.*

## 6. The Hush has faces (the "bosses": encounters, never required)
Encounters are *meetings with the Hush's edge*, each a place where the story is told, not a wall. They can be left; you are never harmed in a way the app records.
- **The Hollow Choir** — Faded singers, the hymn with its verse missing, in the dark of the Archive. *Item:* **the Choirmaster's Breath** — "A breath held for the length of a name."
- **The Unremembered Fleet** — ghost ships, no crews, flying the old lanes from habit. *Item:* **a Faded Ticket** — "Valid for a voyage the holder cannot recall buying."
- **The Quiet Tide** — the Hush's body at the Growing Place, thinning terraces. *Item:* **Pale Leaf** — "A leaf that remembers being a leaf."
- **Vesh at the Front** — the Lullaby, a lullaby, a Firstborn dissolving. *Item:* **Vesh's Paper Boat** — "Folded from a page of something that had to be put down."
- **The Standing** — Eternity rises; the Hush passes through him. *Item:* **A Breath of Morning** — "It has no weight. It remembers being the first."

## 7. Object-text (the world speaks in descriptions) — samples
*(Style: 40–90 words, never explains, always implies; "accounts differ".)*
- **The Choirmaster's Breath.** "A held breath, kept in a glass no bigger than a thumb. The Choir holds it at the end of every hymn for exactly the length of a name, and no one will say whose. Those who have counted it say it is eleven beats. Those who have listened say it is longer."
- **Faded Ticket.** "Valid for a voyage the holder cannot recall buying. The date is smudged by the thumb of someone who kept turning it over. On the back, in a careful hand: *do not let me forget I wanted to go.*"
- **Hesk's Tin Soldier.** "A small tin soldier, one arm missing. It was found in the coat of a person no one remembers, and has been polished every day since by a man who says he has never seen it before."
- **Dorrow's Page.** "A ledger page with a single line: *owed to one unrecorded.* The ink is the freshest in the Book. It is renewed every year, and no clerk will admit to renewing it."
- **Kith's Drawing.** "A woman with no face, sitting at a table set for one more than the family counts. The crayon has been pressed so hard it has torn the paper at the mouth."
- **The Sleepwalker's Lantern.** "She carries it at night. It has no flame. She cannot say why she carries it, and she will not put it down."
- **A Breath of Morning.** "It has no weight. It remembers being the first thing. It does not remember what came after."
- **Vesh's Paper Boat.** "A little boat folded from an Archive page. The page was one that had to be set down, and was. If you hold it to the light you can see that someone has tried to write a name on the inside and stopped after the first stroke."
- **The Doorkeeper's Nail.** "Not a nail; the shape of one. It is warm. It is always warm. It opens nothing, but those who carry it find the next door is a little easier to push."
- **The Measurer's Cord.** "A knotted cord, one knot for every mile she has ever walked. The last knot is untied. When asked why, she says: *because it hasn't ended.*"

## 8. Five Accounts of the Rending (they contradict on purpose)
- **The Choir's.** "Eternity withdrew his voice in grief at the Sixth Hour. The made failed to hold a hymn that could call it back. The verse that was missing was *theirs* to sing."
- **The Open Hand's.** "A woman died and no one knew how to hold the loss. A Firstborn took it from us and we are still carrying what remained."
- **The Ledger's.** "A debt was incurred in the Sixth Hour and never repaid. Everything since has been interest."
- **The Undertow's.** "Nothing happened. The sky was always silent. The hymns are what we told ourselves to fill it."
- **The Wardens'.** "There was a breach. The made were exposed to a harm too great to name. The ordinances are the walls we built and must not drop."
*(All are partly true. None names her.)*

## 9. The Hidden Web (secrets that join the threads)
1. The **Harbor Nail** was set by Calend and handed to Teru; Calend's own nail is *still warm.*
2. The **Mooring Ring** is where Brannoch sat — he had no ship; "tied to nothing" is literal.
3. The **Hollow Hymnal's** missing page and **Dorrow's** unrecorded debt are *the same entry*: the Ledger and the Choir each kept half of one thing.
4. **Hesk's locked room** holds a **tin soldier** that *belongs to Kith* — she is the child of a Faded.
5. **The Sleepwalker's Lantern** is the one Perrin Lampwright kept lit — it was *lent*.
6. **Orsolya's Shard** and **the Unnumbered Fragment** are two halves of **the first record**.
7. **Cael Waterline's tally** of the Basin Stone equals **Tamsin's** count of the Faded.
8. **The empty chair** in every hearth is **Kith's table**.
9. **Maud and Edric's Ember** is warmer when **two** players are in the People world together *(future: shared worlds)*.
10. **The dead bough** points at **the sealed seam**.
11. **Soumaya's gap** is the **same shape** as **Vesh's hull hollow**.
12. The **Sixteenth Doorway** is *already open* — you have been walking toward it since the app first said "welcome back."

## 10. Never say
Her name. Whether she was ever a person. Why he does not speak, plainly. What the Folded One wrote. Whether the Uncounted is Eternity. Whether the player's first memory was hers.
