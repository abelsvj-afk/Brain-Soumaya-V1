## §1 What the interior is

The Station's inside — the same harbor, from within. Not a new world. You fly up to the Station, arrive outside as today, and load into the place.

## §2 Movement — SETTLED

Jump between rooms from a list. No walking. No first-person movement, no free-roam corridors. You pick a room, you are there.

## §3 Rooms — SETTLED (four)

1. **Hangar bay** — pick your ship. (Already settled separately; the one interior space that comes before the interior proper.)
2. **Commission board** — take commissions. The board carries the settled commission scope: Link, Review, Journey step, Inbox item.
3. **Fleet deck** — see your fleet, send fleets on automated missions, fleet defense.
4. **Observation deck** — look out at the galaxy. A quiet place to be. No task, no counter, nothing to earn — the meeting is the reward, same as Eternity's rule for quiet spaces.

No other rooms. No market (trading is out), no bridge, no crew quarters, no docks beyond the hangar bay.

- Form: the Station's inside, per §1. Crown-and-stem megastructure from outside; inside, the four rooms above.
- Palette: inherits the Station recipe — warm amber working light against cold indigo.
