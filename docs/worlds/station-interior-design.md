## §1 What the interior is

The Station's inside — the same harbor, from within. Not a new world. You fly up to the Station, arrive outside as today, and load into the place.

**Arrival is EVE-style — SETTLED.** Your ship flies in through the bay and docks. When you load into the station you are in the hangar bay, with your ship floating in the center of the bay, the way EVE shows your docked ship when you load into a station. The ship is the center of the view; you can look around it.

## §2 Movement — SETTLED

Jump between rooms from a list. No walking. No first-person movement, no free-roam corridors. You pick a room, you are there. (The dock-in of §1 is the arrival into the station, not movement inside it.)

## §3 Rooms — SETTLED (four)

1. **Hangar bay** — the arrival room and the ship room. Your ship flies in, docks, and floats in the center of the bay (EVE-style, §1); you can look around it. You pick and change ships from here. The bay also shows your fleet roster and the ships you have used. No buying, no trading, no fuel exchange.
2. **Commission board** — take commissions. The board carries the settled commission scope: Link, Review, Journey step, Inbox item.
3. **Fleet deck** — see your fleet, send fleets on automated missions, fleet defense.
4. **Observation deck** — look out at the galaxy. A quiet place to be. No task, no counter, nothing to earn — the meeting is the reward, same as Eternity's rule for quiet spaces.

No other rooms. No market (trading is out), no bridge, no crew quarters, no docks beyond the hangar bay.
