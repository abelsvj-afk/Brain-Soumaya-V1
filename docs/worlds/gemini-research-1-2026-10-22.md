## King's critique of the research (what Gemini got wrong or underweighted)

1. **The ISS verdict is too quick.** Gemini crowns the ISS "the clear winner" for the Great Station on efficiency. Efficiency is real (6.6k polys) — but the owner's vision is an EVE-style *monumental fictional megastructure*, and the ISS is a recognizable real-world station with a thin truss silhouette, 109 m long in reality. Scaled up 10,000× it may read as "a model of the ISS, but big," not as an awe landmark — and its real-world identity fights the fiction of the user's own universe. It stays a **leading placeholder candidate**, not a settled winner. A composite (ISS-derived core + modular kit extensions to bulk the silhouette) or reserving the ISS for the reference-scale space (where "real" is the point) are live alternatives — owner's call, queued as Q29.
2. **Two shortlist entries were filler** (museum ring, molecule) — noted and dropped above. A shortlist with verified licenses but wrong objects is worse than a shorter honest one.
3. **The legal framing erred toward fear in one place (HYG) and imprecision in another (ESA/Hubble)** — both corrected above. License hygiene means *accurate* terms, not scariest terms.
4. **What the research genuinely settles:** the *method* — seeded palette math, layered nebula planes on an equirect background sphere (not hi-res cubemaps), instancing everywhere, fog as glue, dual-layer scale illusion instead of logarithmic depth, baked-in glow. That stack is coherent, cheap, and matches the environment-recipe model from ChatGPT's review. It converts Q25 from "which method?" into a specific proposed stack for the owner to ratify.

## Design consequences (carried into Exploration Revision 3, §10)

- **Scale rendering decision-shaped:** felt scale via dual-layer compositing (background layer: celestial bodies at compressed scale; foreground layer: local objects 1:1; depth cleared between) — the sun can "keep growing" on approach in the background layer without depth-precision collapse. This is the leading answer to Q8–Q10's implementation half; the *design* half (what lives there) stays open.
- **Minimal real-data set, corrected:** HYG brightest ~5,000 stars → static JSON, B−V → RGB, point-cloud shader, attribution in credits + derived file under CC BY-SA 4.0 · JPL SBDB curated 50–100 named asteroids → static JSON at build time (API never called from the client) · Skies: NASA imagery (public domain, insignia-free selections) and ESA/Hubble (CC BY 4.0, visible credit) as equirect backdrops, per-source terms checked at use.
- **Asset register (verified entries only):** NASA ISS (public domain, insignia audit required) · Poly Haven Boulder 01 (CC0, use low LOD, 1K textures) · Kenney kits (CC0 as a publisher; exact kit/page to confirm at download time).
