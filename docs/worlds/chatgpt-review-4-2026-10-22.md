## Recommendations
| Question | ChatGPT recommends |
|---|---|
| Q-M1 Presentation bounds | Keep the structural/presentation distinction, **stricter**: allowed = palette/weather within the established recipe, ambient traffic/drift, reversible emphasis and display ordering. Rail-required = landmarks, confirmed connections, clusters, anchors, primary homes, persistent chapter/tour membership. Four safeguards: no hidden structural effects; explainable; reversible and controllable (reset/disable without touching memories); never-punish (no decay, shame, ominous deterioration, productivity pressure, no implying an emotional state is established fact). **Subtlety:** ordering is presentation only as a temporary display choice — rewriting a saved tour/chapter sequence is structural. |
| Q-M2 Cadence | **Batch into a calm review moment** — mail, not interruptions. No per-proposal interruption, no urgency/countdown, no approval inferred from silence/inactivity/full queue; approve/edit/reject/**defer**; under cap pressure wait quietly (don't discard, don't force). A calm pending indicator + review view answers the "overlooked proposal" counterargument. |
| Q-M3 Undo horizon | **No time-based permanence**; append-only log; compensating entries without expiration. **Precision:** undo must not blindly restore an old snapshot over newer dependent changes — calculate a safe compensation, or explain the conflict and ask the owner; never silently overwrite subsequent work. Distinguish logical reversibility from physical erasure. |
| Q-M4 Log's home | **Both**: on-demand explanations at point of use + browsable "World Changes" view (proposals and outcomes: approvals, recorded rejections, applied changes, compensating entries), plain language, brain's naming, owner's local time. Distinct from the Chronicle (user's life vs. what the world did and why). The view is a reference/control surface, not a notification stream. Both doors share one log and one explanation ruleset. |

## Cross-cutting issue (recorded in §17 M1 as an invariant; mechanism deferred)
M1's atomic apply is the right invariant, but the design should eventually define partway-failure behavior. Required outcome fixed now: **no partial structural mutation, no false "applied" log entry**. Mechanism belongs to the later implementation/testing specification; settled steps are not reopened for it.
