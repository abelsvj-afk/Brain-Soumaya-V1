# The Man-God World — Owner Concept Note (brainstorm stage, 2026-10-23)

Status: **owner-directed addition to the worlds plan, CONCEPT STAGE.** No design gate, no build authorization. Recorded from the owner's own description; open questions and builder pushbacks are marked as such. Placement in the build order is unsettled — by its nature (shared, multi-visitor) it stacks on the shared-presence track, which is itself design-only today.

## The concept (owner's words, organized)

- **A world you travel to — the furthest world reachable.** Getting there is meant to be very hard, very difficult, very challenging: the long journey is part of its meaning.
- **The being:** a gigantic cosmic god on a Marvel-Celestial scale, standing in the world while ships float around him. His FORM is inspired by the Man-God of *Mushoku Tensei* — a completely white, featureless humanoid silhouette — at cosmic scale. For the owner, his face is Jesus: the being stands for Christ, and the world exists partly to show what these worlds and this app are for — how they help people.
- **He barely moves, and movement is monumental.** When he moves a hand, things happen on a world scale (the owner's example: a ship is destroyed and blows up — everyone present sees it).
- **A shared world.** Many people can be there at once, each in their own ship (choosing different ships is a future feature). Visitors do NOT all receive the same message from him — **each person can receive a different, personal message — but everyone sees the same emotion and the same animations.** The animation must relate to the message each person received (animation↔message coherence). Sometimes he may choose for everyone to see the same message.
- **He has a real job.** He is not decoration: he serves a function in how the app helps people.
- **He is automated — an agent, not a script.** He will have his own API key and model access (Gemini or the existing key to start; upgraded models later), and **his own tools**, the way Soumaya has tools — tools that let him interact with and manipulate the worlds in certain ways.

## Design seeds (builder's, from the concept)

- **Emotion is the broadcast layer; the message is the private layer.** The workable contract: a small vocabulary of monumental states/gestures (stillness, regard, sorrow, joy, warning, blessing…). Every message he sends carries one of these tags; all visitors render the same tagged gesture at the same moment, while the words stay personal. Coherence is then guaranteed by construction, not hoped for.
- **His job, candidate shape:** he speaks to what a visitor brings — guidance, blessing, challenge, comfort — drawn (with consent) from what their own brain surfaces. His tools would be world-actions only (speak, gesture, move, reorder light/scenery), never brain-writes.
- **Arrival:** "furthest" fits the travel economy honestly — a long chain of journeys through the other worlds; effort in the fiction, never a paywall (D-1: fuel buys speed, never access; home always free).

## Pushbacks recorded (owner invited them; his calls to settle)

1. **The name carries the wrong story.** In *Mushoku Tensei*, the Man-God is a manipulative deceiver who uses people. If this being's face is Jesus, shipping him under a villain's name invites exactly the wrong reading — especially in a faith-forward app. *Recommendation: keep the form (featureless white colossus), choose his name deliberately as his own.*
2. **Destruction vs the never-punish invariant. — SETTLED (owner, 2026-10-23): destruction is OUT.** The owner agreed and withdrew it ("Forget the destruction"); his power reads through wonder, protection, and world-scale reordering instead, and his tools should feel cosmic in scale while staying inside the fence (D-54). The original flag stands below for the record. The founding rule of these worlds: the world never punishes — no loss, no shame, nothing real is ever taken. A god who blows up a visitor's ship collides with that head-on, and with failure-independence (the worlds layer must never be able to harm the brain or the person's real progress). *Recommendation: his monumental acts are wondrous, protective, or reordering — or any "destruction" is purely theatrical, consented, and instantly restored, with nothing real ever lost.* The owner's example stands in this note as his raw concept; the invariant is flagged against it for settlement.
3. **Shared means later.** Current scope is private single-user (D-24); shared presence is a separate, design-only track. This world is inherently shared, so it cannot jump the queue ahead of that infrastructure — its "furthest world" nature suits it to arriving late in the order anyway.
4. **An autonomous agent needs a fence, not just a key.** His tools must be allow-listed world-actions (the §16 ship-agent pattern), with anything structural going through propose→decide (§17) — he never writes to a brain, never reaches outside his world uninvited, and his spending runs under a budget. Power with a fence is what makes him trustworthy enough to meet.

## Owner direction 2026-10-25 — the name, the flicker, the alignment, the projection (his words, organized)

- **The name is not "Man-God."** The owner delegated the pick to King: something more cosmic and godly, Marvel-universe-like. **King's pick: ETERNITY** — Marvel's Eternity, the living embodiment of the universe, the one who sees all. It fits him: timeless, cosmic, omniscient by nature, not a villain's name. Recorded as King's pick at the owner's delegation — the owner can correct it with a word. His face, for the owner, is still Jesus; the name Eternity does not conflict with that.
- **He does not have to be approached or spoken to first.** He can act and speak first — initiation is his (settles NPC-framework Q2: he CAN initiate). But he does not always speak. He speaks rarely, when a **cosmic alignment** takes place.
- **Cosmic alignment (what earns regular travel to him).** Alignment is the energy a person's own memory galaxy exhibits — how and what their memories say about the person they belong to: emotionally, mentally, goal-wise — and, in a very scarce, slim amount, the energy certain tabs in the app feed. When that energy aligns with how he likes it, the visitor gains the ability to travel to him regularly. This is earned through the character of one's own memories, never bought (D-1: fuel buys speed, never access), never a score, never a shame signal — the alignment mechanic is a design question for his world's design note, and it must be built so it can never read as a ranking or a judgment.
- **Real space flickers into his world.** From time to time, real space flickers into his world — brief, ambient glimpses of the real universe. That is how he shows that he sees all. Presentation only: never interactive, never a signal about the visitor, never a claim the flicker means anything beyond his seeing.
- **He can project himself into any brain, on a cosmic scale.** His projection can appear in any visitor's world, so big you would not see or be able to fly to the bottom of his feet. This is the one intentional exception to the completeness principle: his form has no reachable bottom *by design* — the unreachable bottom is the point (he is that large), not a flaw. The camera cannot reach it because he is that big, not because it was left unfinished.
- **Autonomous, free will, with guardrails on the backend.** At the end of the day he is autonomous and can act on free will — inside the fence (§16/§17: allow-listed world-actions, propose→decide for structural acts, never writes to a brain, budgeted, kill switch, audit log). Free will within the fence is the whole design.
- **Think of him like Jesus — like the Trinity, all in one — with a mix of Marvel comics (owner direction, 2026-10-25).** He is one being, three-in-one: like the Trinity, three persons in one God — and his face is Jesus. The Marvel mix flavors the cosmic presentation. **This chat does not design him: his form, his aspects, and his implementation already exist in the other chat — in code and in a mockup there.** This note records only the owner's direction; the other chat holds the design.
- **The lore — he is, and always was (owner direction, 2026-10-25).** The beginning happened because of him, not the other way around. He created all the other worlds — the ones that exist now and the ones added in the future. His lore has to run as deep as humanly possible: it is the mystery layer outside the app's other elements, something to chase and have fun with. Full lore bible: `docs/worlds/eternity-lore.md` (DRAFT — the owner's lore, structured by King; he settles it). Rule going forward: every new world gets its creation story in its design note.
