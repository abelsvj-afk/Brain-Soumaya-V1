## 1. Identity & visual grammar

**One-line identity:** *The deep water where your earlier life is kept — everything down here is still yours, still lit, still reachable.*

**Form (King's proposal, owner settles):** a **descending spiral of strata** — a vast, slow helix canyon whose terraced bands wind downward around a warm-lit core. Each band is a **stratum**: a ring-terrace of kept memories resting in alcoves of dark stone and soft light. Depth means *earlier* (by the D-7 rule, §3) — the further down, the longer ago — and the descent is the space's whole spatial sentence: you don't browse the past here, you *go down to it*, and going down is gentle. At the center-bottom the spiral opens into the **First Light basin**: the oldest stratum, held in the warmest glow — the bottom of this world is not an ending or a pit; it is where the light is oldest and steadiest.

**Seed & palette:**
- **Seed string:** `deep-past / first-descent-1` — stratum tinting, particulate, alcove scatter derive deterministically from it. The helix architecture and depth grammar are fixed form language.
- **Key light:** memory-light itself — each resting memory carries its own small warm lantern-glow (real: the glow marks a real resting member, §3); ambient light is deep **violet dusk**, low and calm, brightening subtly with depth toward the basin's ember warmth. Distinct on sight from harbor amber (industrial, horizontal) and garden dawn (green, upward): this world is *violet, vertical, and quiet*.
- **Backdrop:** no sky — the helix encloses; beyond the strata, darkness textured with slow particulate drift (marine snow: atmosphere, §3) and the faintest HYG points far above the rim, where the present day lives.
- **Silhouette:** the lit spiral itself — a descending ribbon of warm alcove-lights winding into violet depth. From the rim, the whole shape reads in one look: *a long way down, and lit the whole way.*

**The depth rule (this space's constitution):** depth is **never** neglect. A memory resting deep is not buried, lost, fading, or lesser — it is *kept at depth*, with the same light and the same reachability as anything at the rim. Nothing down here dims with age, nothing sinks on its own, and no visual ever suggests that what is old matters less. The worst-week test (§9.4): a user who hasn't touched an old memory in years must find it here *exactly as kept* — lit, named, one explanation away from its story.

**Chronological monotonicity (the depth rule's safeguard — review, 2026-10-22):** depth is chronology, **not a score**. Age establishes a memory's chronological position, and that ordering is **monotonic**: resurfacing context determines *membership* and may bring a memory forward (the rise, §3), but context can **never** make a newer memory appear chronologically older than an earlier one. No emotional inference, visit frequency, productivity measure, or inactivity may push a memory deeper or make it less visible. The visual metaphor may never contradict the data.

## 2. Spatial composition & the mockup scene

The mockup scene (§18 V1) is **the descent, in Chronicle guided mode**, in three movements:

1. **The rim:** arrival above the helix — the spiral of lit strata turning away below, the basin's warmth at the center of the depth. The Chronicle path is visible as a **thread of slightly brighter light** winding down the strata ahead of the ship: the route, made visible before it is traveled (§15 N7; ship-agent queue input 1 — narration traceable to real node content).
2. **The descent (Chronicle guided):** the ship follows the thread down at journey pace, passing alcoves; at each **stop** (a real memory on the route) the thread brightens, the alcove answers, and the memory is visitable — narration drawn from the memory's own content, never invented color (§16 honesty). The user can leave the guided path at any stop and fly free; the thread remains, patient, rejoinable.
3. **The placement (full A1–A3):** at the route's end, the carried memory — brought down as visible cargo through the whole descent (A2) — is settled into its alcove (A3): the alcove light warms to receive it, the memory comes to rest at its D-7 depth, and the Chronicle thread dims back to ambient. This is the only mockup of the three that exercises the *entire* carry → transit → placement chain inside its signature scene (§18).

**Composition rules:** the helix winds one consistent direction (descent never confuses up with down); depth cues are redundant — stratum banding, particulate density, light temperature — so depth reads even in reduced motion and for color-vision differences; strata are **bands, not bins**: boundaries are soft light-gradients, because D-7 membership is a rule with context, not a date guillotine (§3).

## 3. Meaningful content & visual honesty

- **Membership (D-7, made visible):** a memory rests here when the explainable rule places it — age *combined with* meaningful resurfacing context — and every resting memory can answer "why are you in the Deep Past?" in one plain line (P6), naming the rule inputs, with the user's controls (thresholds, manual include/exclude, D-7) reachable from that explanation. **Never** classified here for age alone under a label like "forgotten" — that word does not exist in this space's vocabulary.
- **Depth encodes the rule, nothing else:** resting depth derives from the same explainable inputs (how long kept, the resurfacing context) by a fixed mapping the user can read — depth is *never* a function of neglect, visit frequency, or emotional inference (§17 M5: inferred feeling moves weather, never geography — and never depth).
- **Anchors (D-8):** a member with a confirmed anchor elsewhere shows its link as a fine line of light rising out of the depth toward its other appearance — the primary home stays put; the line is a visit-path, not a relocation.
- **Chronicle routes are real routes:** the thread exists only where a saved route exists (§15 N7); its stops are the route's real memories in the route's real order; narration is traceable to node content — where content is thin, the narration is thin, honestly, rather than padded.
- **Resurfacing:** when life touches a deep memory again (it is linked, visited from elsewhere, or its context returns), the memory **rises** — a gentle ascent to a rim-side resting place while its context is warm. **What the rise is, precisely (review constraint):** a *derived, explainable change in presentation within the Deep Past* — **not** a change to the canonical memory, its primary home, its chronological stratum, or its historical identity. It never duplicates the memory and never creates a partially applied structural change (§14 atomicity). Its cause is visible ("risen because…", P6), and its return to depth follows the **same explainable rule** that raised it — never a neglect timer (§1's monotonicity safeguard).
- **Atmosphere (never fact):** marine-snow particulate, slow light-breathing in alcoves, the basin's ember shimmer — presentation within §17 M2; none of it implies activity, visits, or change that didn't happen.

Violet/ember palette checked so depth and selection never depend on hue alone (band shape, light rhythm, labels). Vertigo care: descent speed is capped and user-controlled; the guided mode can pause at any stop indefinitely — the deep never hurries anyone.
