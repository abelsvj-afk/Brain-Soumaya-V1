## Design check (absorbed into §15 N3/N6)
N3 (return home always free) and N6 (mid-transit exit returns to origin) are compatible but must distinguish three acts:
- **Cancel before departure:** nothing moves.
- **Turn back during transit:** return to origin, operation state preserved.
- **Return home:** always available, always free, even without fuel.
Critical with cargo: a travel cancellation must never accidentally relocate, archive, or duplicate a carried memory.
