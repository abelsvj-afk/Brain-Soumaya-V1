# Station Interior — Scope Note (DRAFT)

**Status:** DRAFT — filed 2026-10-26 (Rev 123). **Scope only. DO NOT BUILD HERE.** This note exists to hold the boundary so the interior is neither built by accident nor forgotten.
**What this note is:** the scoping of the Station interior as a later phase. It records the promise, the boundary, and the conditions for revisiting — and explicitly authorizes nothing.
**Settled ground it obeys:** D-2 (the interior is a later phase, not now); Q23 (promise approach + destination only); the completeness principle (every reachable angle finished, or unreachable); the never-punish invariant; D-1 (fuel buys speed, never access).

---

## §1 The promise today — approach + destination only

- **What the Station is today:** a place you fly to and arrive at. The approach (three-stage: silhouette → handoff → arrival arc) and the arrival are the whole promise. Arrival is a *place you reach* — the ring, the docking arms, the traffic, the gate markers, the route projections. **No docking capture, no interior.**
- **What the promise is for later (EVE-style):** fly up to the Station, and eventually load into the place — step inside the harbor you have been flying around. The interior is the Station's inside, not a new world: the same harbor, from within.
- **The promise is honest today:** nothing in the current Station implies an interior that isn't there. No door that won't open, no window into a room that doesn't exist, no "coming soon" sign. An angle that cannot be finished is outside the allowed range.

## §2 What is NOT designed here

- **No layout.** No rooms, no corridors, no docks, no market, no bridge, no quarters. Nothing about what is inside.
- **No interactions.** No docking capture, no walking, no talking to the harbor controller inside (the controller meets you at arrival, outside — see `docs/worlds/npc-autonomy-design-note.md`).
- **No hangar interior beyond the bay.** The hangar (see `docs/worlds/ship-picker-hangar-design-note.md`) is a bay you enter to change ships — it is the one interior space that exists before the interior proper, and it is deliberately a bay, not the Station's inside.
- **No budget, no recipe, no build.** This note sets no numbers and authorizes no work.

## §3 Conditions for revisiting

- The interior is revisited **only after** the six worlds (Station, Growing Place, Deep Past, People, Scale, Quiet/Archive) + Eternity's world + the hangar are built and gated — and only on the owner's word. "Maybe not yet" was his word (inventory (b) #21); the answer to "when" is his.
- When it is revisited, it gets its own design note (form, palette, layout, interactions, budget, acceptance) through the same lane as every other world — design first, review, then build. This scope note does not become that design note by being edited; the design note is written fresh, on the evidence of the built Station.

## §4 What the build coordinators should do with this note

- **Nothing.** Do not build the interior, do not scaffold it, do not leave hooks for it beyond what the Station's existing modules already provide. If a build decision touches the interior boundary (a camera range, a collision surface, a door-shaped piece of geometry), the decision is: the boundary stays closed — the angle is unreachable, the geometry is finished from outside.
